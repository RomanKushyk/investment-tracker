Interactions to verify:
1. Type into an empty input → its delta chip computes live vs yesterday's value; pill count increments ("2 of 4 filled"). Comma and dot decimals both accepted.
2. Typed drafts survive a reload (persisted draft store).
3. "Copy yesterday" fills all 4 inputs with yesterday's quotes.
4. "Save snapshot" → toast **"Snapshot saved"**, "Last saved" updates, IndexedDB row for the date is UPSERTED (re-saving the same day must not add a row).
5. Transaction form: selecting Asset = "+ New asset…" reveals the dashed **New asset details** sub-card; any other asset hides it. Submitting with a new asset creates asset + transaction atomically → toast **"Transaction recorded"**, Recent list updates, new asset appears as a 5th quote row with a cycled avatar tint. *Since next-phase P2 the sub-card renders the shared AssetForm fields (create mode, no First purchase — still derived from the transaction date): Name, Code (avatar preview, auto-derived from Name until edited), Yield type, Expected/Target, Payout schedule (never 'none'), plus the conditional Fixed-coupon group (yield type = Fixed coupon) and the "Link to Inzhur" toggle (off by default). The atomic `recordTransaction(tx, newAsset)` path is unchanged.*
6. **Accept / dismiss a suggestion (S4):** press "Use suggested …?" → the input becomes a real draft with that exact value (green border, ink text), the pill collapses and the progress pill increments; the draft survives a reload with provenance `accrual` in `quirenote-draft`.origins (no chip renders in demo — S2 keeps chips to Inzhur-linked rows in live). Press `✕` instead → the ghost is gone for the selected date and the input is a plain empty one; picking another date re-offers it. Typing over a ghost clears it on the first keystroke.
7. **Record a coupon (S5) — exactly one write, one roll:** with a due coupon, press **Record coupon** → toast **"Coupon recorded"** ("Coupon + reinvest recorded" with the checkbox on), the card collapses, and IndexedDB gains **exactly one** `interest_payout` dated the COUPON's date (`source: 'accrual'`, "Coupon · …8976" in Recent transactions) plus the asset's `nextCoupon` advanced **once** by the schedule **from the occurrence just recorded** (clamped onto `maturity`; at maturity it is flagged and stops suggesting). **Double-click the button** — still one transaction and one roll (StrictMode-safe: the write lives in the click handler behind a ref latch). After a reload the card does not return: a recorded payout within ±7 days of the date dedupes the occurrence — and if the NEXT scheduled date is also already in the past, its card appears immediately (the grid walk, D23). Empty the amount → neg border + **"Enter an amount."** (wired to the input via `aria-describedby`) and nothing is written. NO `tax` row is ever created (G5/D13).
8. **Skip (S5) writes nothing, and the next date still suggests:** press **Skip** → the card collapses, the transaction count is unchanged, `nextCoupon` is unchanged, and `quirenote-settings.state.dismissedReminders` gains `coupon:<assetId>:<date>`. The NEXT coupon date suggests normally — to see it in one step, set a bond to **Monthly** with a "Next coupon" two months in the past: skipping the first card immediately raises a card (and an overdue banner) for the following month. Restore every dismissal via Settings→Automation → "Restore dismissed" (checkpoint 19).
9. **Dismiss a banner (S6):** press a banner ✕ ("Dismiss reminder") → it fades/slides out over ~220ms and the banners below move up; `quirenote-settings.state.dismissedReminders` gains the banner's DERIVED id (`quote-missing:<date>` / `coupon:<assetId>:<date>` / `coupon-overdue:<assetId>:<date>` / `maturity:<assetId>:<date>`) and it stays hidden **across a reload**. Ids expire by themselves: tomorrow's `quote-missing` is a new id, and so is the next coupon date. Restore = Settings→Automation. Under `prefers-reduced-motion` the banner disappears instantly (the write still happens).
10. **Skipping a coupon card also silences its overdue banner** (shared derived id, D21/D22): press **Skip** on an overdue coupon's S5 card → the matching `coupon-overdue` banner is gone too. Dismissing the BANNER does not remove the card.

Until A47 the field kept its text while the form's state was empty, so the second press was refused with «Перевірте підсвічені поля» and a reload was the only way out. **Empty the amount and press**: neg border + **«Введіть суму.»** under the field (linked by `aria-describedby`, NOT folded into the label), the summary line above it, and exactly **one** `aria-invalid` element on the page — a summary that names highlights must have one. **Type `0`, `-500` or `abc` instead**: «Сума має бути додатним числом.», because one sentence for all four of `quoteInputSchema`'s refusals told someone who typed `0` to enter the amount they had just typed. **Asset = «+ Новий актив…» with the sub-form empty**: 4 highlighted fields, not 0. **Press three times fast**: ONE row — the submit path is latched, and `disabled={isPending}` never covered the window where `handleSubmit` is still awaiting its resolver.

- **«Одиниці» + the Σ/1 toggle (issue #31, D112; variant A, 2026-08-31).** The toggle rides the amount field's own LABEL ROW, right-aligned, at the **Settings switch's footprint since D114** — track 40 × 22 radius 7, `bg-ink` with a `bg-card` sliding chip, segments 15 × 16 radius 4 and no padding of their own. **It is under WCAG 2.5.8 AA and that is on the record, not a regression to report**: 26.6 × 24.5 cleared 24 × 24 before, `TAP_44` cannot cover a 19px pitch, and the owner asked for the footprint knowing it — `Σ` for the whole transaction, `1` for one unit, each carrying its words in `title` and `aria-label` since the glyph is not a name. **`₴` was rejected as the total's glyph:** both modes are in hryvnia, so it distinguished nothing; Σ against 1 compares an operation with an operation. **THE ROW HAS TWO SHAPES** (owner's rulings, 2026-08-31). With units: `[Одиниці][Сума]` and «Джерело коштів» full width beneath, the shape «Актив» above already uses. Without them: `[Сума][Джерело коштів]` on one line — the row closes up rather than leaving a hole, which is what this form looked like before the count existed. All three are auto-placed; the only rule is `group-has-[#tx-quantity]:col-span-2` on «Джерело», and a spanning cell cannot fit beside two others, so the span alone pushes it to its own line. **It asks the DOM, not `takesUnits`** — the flag flips while the field is still playing its leave animation, so a layout driven by it would reflow twice; `:has` is false only once the node is gone. The row is also a THREE-ROW SUBGRID — label, control, error — so its cells align control-to-control whatever sits above or below them: the segment makes the amount's label row the taller one, and an earlier `items-end` held only until an error rendered and put the two 36px controls 20.5px out of line (measured). «Джерело» never sits beside a «Сума» carrying the track, since the track is gated on the same condition. **Measured in both shapes: inputs and labels aligned at 0px.** Both appear only on a type that MOVES A POSITION — Купівля, Продаж, Реінвест, Погашення — and slide away on Внесок / Виплата / Податок, which is `transaction_quantity_absent_ck` shown rather than only enforced. **Walk it:** pick Купівля, enter `5 000` units, leave the toggle on **Загальна** and type `55 694,50` → the row records `amount 55694.5`, `quantity 5000`, `unitPrice 11.1389`. Flip the toggle to **1**: the amount label becomes **«За одиницю, ₴»** — the label is the contract, and a toggle that changed a number's meaning silently would be worse than the bug it fixes. Type `11,1389` with the same 5 000 → the same three stored values. **Empty the units in «За одиницю» mode**: «Введіть кількість одиниць.» — the one failure the mode adds, since without a count there is no total. **Switch the type to Податок while units are filled**: the field clears itself on the way out, because a hidden field holding a value is an invisible error. After a successful record **«Одиниці» clears with «Сума» but the toggle does NOT** — the count is per-transaction, the mode is how this user reads their statements.
- **Units are DERIVED from here, not from the asset (D112).** `/` → «Отримати котирування» values a linked position as `Σ quantity × sell price`. Until a position's rows carry quantities it falls back to the asset form's stored units — the pre-#31 behaviour, kept because that total is the only seed for the W7 backfill — and `unitsFrom` records which of the two answered.
