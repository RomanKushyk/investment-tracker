## D72 — "the provider publishes no price history" is too strong — narrows D27

**2026-08-18.** The sentence that justifies the whole of `infra/` was written
from an assumption and has now been answered by the provider and by the archive.
It is not wrong so much as **unscoped**, and unscoped is what made it load-bearing
in places it should not have been.

**What is actually true, by category:**

| | history available? |
|---|---|
| Funds (REIT, Energy), to **June 2026** | **YES** — Excel files in the open on the Inzhur site, among the funds' documents |
| Funds, after June 2026 | No. It moved into the mobile app as a chart; an API for clients is **in their backlog** |
| Bonds, NBU **fair value** | **YES, AND ALREADY CAPTURED** — see below |
| Bonds, Inzhur's **dealer quote** | No, and nowhere: it is a dealer's price, not a registry fact |

**The bond half was already solved and nobody noticed.** `price_observation`
holds `UA4000236475` from **2025-07-23** (279 rows) and `UA4000238976` from
**2026-02-04** (139 rows) — each from its own issuance — derived from 3 861
`nbu_fv` captures reaching back to 2016-01-04. Looking for bond history anywhere
else is work already done.

**But the two are NOT substitutes, and D26/D27 already ruled on that.** NBU
publishes a modelled fair value; Inzhur publishes a dealer quote ("Базова ціна",
cl. 1.4 of its services agreement). Measured divergence is ~0.9 % same-day on the
same ISIN. So "we have NBU history" does not mean "we have the price the app
shows", and merging them stays forbidden.

**What the daily capture still buys, stated narrowly:** the Inzhur dealer quote
for every instrument, which exists nowhere else and begins 2026-08-11; and fund
prices after June 2026, until their API ships. **That is a smaller claim than
"the provider publishes no history" and it is the true one.** D27's ruling — that
there is no single source of truth for prices and the axis is backfillability —
is untouched and is if anything better supported now.

**The files must be fetched BY HAND, and that is settled by evidence already in
the repository.** `robots.txt` disallows `/documents`, which is where they live
(`infra/README.md`, checked 2026-08-14). The same note records refusing to crawl
`/terms` for exactly this reason: reading it with a crawler while arguing that we
respect their crawl rules would be self-refuting. The owner opening his own
fund's documents is not a crawler; an automated fetch would be.

**One thing this makes possible.** The owner has kept a manual spreadsheet since
**2026-04-23**, which overlaps the Excel files by two months. That overlap is not
redundancy — it is the check that our reading of the files is right before
years of history are trusted.

**Where the old phrasing survives as record and is deliberately not edited:**
D27 itself, the D49/D50 reasoning, `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-08-04-cloud-stack-and-cost.md`,
and `PLAN-OPEN.md`'s dev-archive argument — whose conclusion does not depend on
the scope of the premise. Corrected in the three places that *instruct* rather
than record: `CLAUDE.md`, `infra/README.md` and the `payload_gzip` comment in
`migrations/001_price_capture.sql`.
