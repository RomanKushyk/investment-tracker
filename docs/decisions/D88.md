## D88

**`/` and `/transactions` are composed like `/payouts`, and that supersedes two
merged drawings (2026-08-25)**

The owner, looking at A44 the moment it landed: *«Екран відрізняється від решти
сторінок застосунку, краще використати компонування як у payouts»* — and then,
for `/transactions`, *«таку ж ідіому (справа наліво)»*.

**What is superseded, and it is not a small thing.** `design/extensions/screen-density.dc.html`
§ S1-B drew `/` as a centred 944 composition — 560 + 24 + 360 — and A44 shipped
it, measured to the pixel. `design/extensions/where-things-live.dc.html` § S4
drew `/transactions` with the form in a 360 column on the LEFT and the ledger
taking the rest, and A35 shipped that. Both are merged drawings, which D14 makes
immutable and gives the last word on visual disputes.

**The owner outranks D14, and his reason is why.** D14 exists so that an
implementer cannot re-litigate a drawing; it was never meant to bind the person
the drawing is for. What he saw is something no design session could see from
inside its own sheet: **both screens read as a different product from the rest of
the app.** Every other page is a `1.6fr 1fr` grid at main's own width — `/payouts`
is the reference — and these two were a centred flex composition with container
queries. A screen that is locally optimal and globally foreign is the worse
outcome.

**So the measure changed and the findings did not.** What the two sessions
settled stays settled: `/`'s rail is permanent, its title block sits outside the
column, the yield teaser is a card rather than an eleven-line ribbon at 360, and
`/transactions`' form is the NARROW column with the ledger the wide one — the
half A32 got wrong. Only the geometry that framed them is `/payouts`' now: main's
width, `grid-cols-[1.6fr_1fr]`, `items-start`, `gap-3.5`, one column below `lg`.
`/transactions` is mirrored so the wide track leads, content before side blocks,
which is what every other page does.

**Two consequences, both measured, both recorded because they are the price.**

1. **The void is back to 238,9 px** on a plain quote row (245,5 on a bond row),
   from 115,8. The sheet's whole argument for 560 was that number, and a 683 track
   gives it back. The complaint that opened the brief was that `/` looked *empty*;
   the answer to it now is the populated side column, not a narrow row. If the
   void has to shrink again, the in-row lever is the input's 160 — not the
   composition.
2. **A container query with no container evaluates FALSE and says nothing.** The
   ledger's tall scroll box was keyed to `@min-[944px]` on a row that is no longer
   a `@container`, so it silently sat at the phone's 420 on a desktop. Found by
   measuring, not by reading. Both screens are now keyed to `lg` — the grid's own
   breakpoint — and `transactions-layout.test.ts` pins that no flex basis,
   container query or `@container` is left behind on either screen.

**What this decision does NOT do.** It does not touch the sheets: a merged
drawing is a record of what was decided then, and editing one to match a later
ruling would destroy the only evidence of what changed. It also does not make
`/payouts` the answer for every future screen by itself — it makes the app's
existing idiom the default, and a screen that needs to leave it needs a reason
written down.
