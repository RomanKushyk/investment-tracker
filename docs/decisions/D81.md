## D81

**Every insight card that summarises the bars reads the same ledger the bars
do; the one that describes a schedule does not.** A42, 2026-08-24.

The merged sheet's spine classifies `/seasonality`'s BARS — actual FLOW,
expected FORECAST — and stops there. The three cards beneath them are this
repo's to classify, and leaving it implicit produced a screen that disagreed
with itself inside one viewport.

- **«Якір доходу» WINDOWS.** It names a day derived from the windowed bars, so
  its growth figures must come from the same side of the boundary: at full
  history «580 ₴ → 700 ₴», under `3 місяці` «472 ₴ → 700 ₴». Unwindowed it put
  February's dividend in one breath with a day derived from May onward.
- **«Тиха смуга» WINDOWS**, for the same reason and with a caveat worth writing
  down: a narrow window manufactures the quiet it reports. That is accepted —
  a card that summarises the bars must agree with them — and it is why the
  claim is phrased as an observation rather than a rule.
- **«Купонний сезон» IS HALF-HISTORICAL, and that half windows.** Calling it a
  schedule and leaving it whole was too clean: `bondCouponInfo`'s months are the
  months a bond HAS PAID in, union the one `nextCoupon` names. Only the second
  is a forecast. Unwindowed, the card headlined «лютий і серпень» while the
  chart drew no лютий bar, because лютий came from a payout the same screen had
  just excluded.

**The rule generalises past this screen:** a derived statement inherits the
classification of the data it is derived FROM, not the classification of the
sentence it is written as. A card about "seasons" sounds like a forecast and is
mostly a sum.

**And the attribution follows the same rule.** A bar's colour comes from
`dominantAssetOnDay`, which is a FLOW question — which asset paid most on this
day-of-month — so a windowed height may not wear an unwindowed hue.
