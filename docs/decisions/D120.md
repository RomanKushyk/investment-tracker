## D120 — YTM at purchase, derived (2026-08-31)

[`D119`](D119.md) declined to fold `expectedPct` into the coupon rate, and recorded
the better form of the owner's instinct: the number is not redundant, it is
**derivable**. This makes it so.

```
derivedYtmPct = impliedYield(pricePaid, providerSchedule, firstPurchase)
```

Every piece already existed. `impliedYield` is `dcf.ts`'s bisection inverse, shipped
for the stale-quote check; `purchaseUnitPrice` reads the `unitPrice` that
[#31](https://github.com/RomanKushyk/investment-tracker/issues/31) put on the buy
transaction; `firstPurchase` every asset has.

**Three inputs, each of which can be absent**, and `undefined` when any is:

| piece | absent when |
|---|---|
| price | the purchase predates #31 — those rows record ₴ and nothing else |
| schedule | the bond is unlinked, or the feed does not carry it |
| date | never — `firstPurchase` is required |

The stored `expectedPct` renders instead. That is a fallback to the only number
available, not to a worse one: an unlinked bond has no schedule and nothing can
invent one.

**The EARLIEST purchase, not an average.** "At purchase" names one moment, and the
field it derives is dated by `firstPurchase`. Averaging a ladder of buys would print
a yield no single trade ever had.

**Never stored.** The same rule `dcf.ts` already states for a derived price: the
premises are captured forever, the conclusion never is ([`D31`](D31.md)). A stored
YTM goes stale the moment the provider revises the schedule, and nothing would say
so.

`/attributes` reads whatever feed the app already holds — live payload if a fetch
has run, otherwise the last-good cache — and **does not trigger a fetch**. It is a
reference table; a page that quietly calls the provider on open is what the picker's
"first open, never on mount" rule exists to prevent.

## `targetPct` says what it is

The form asked for «Ціль, %» / "Target, %". Every other surface calls it «Цільова
частка», and it drives `/allocation`'s entire rebalance plan — `sharePct`,
`allocationDeltaPp`, `trimAmount`, `topUpAmount`. Renamed to «Цільова частка, %» /
"Target share, %".

## Recorded, not acted on

Two questions were researched in the same pass and came back differently from how
they were framed. Both are left for a ruling.

**`maturity` and `nextCoupon` are not inert — they are among the most load-bearing
fields a bond has.** `nextCoupon` anchors the coupon grid (`couponsInGap`), starts
the unsettled walk (`nextUnsettledCoupon`), is the projection's date
(`couponProjection`), and is what the S5 confirm rolls. `maturity` stops the ghost
accrual (`suggestedQuote`), clamps the roll and produces `{kind:'matured'}`, backs
the projection date, and raises the **maturity reminder**. Remove either and the
coupon card, the payout projection and the seasonality bars go with it.

**What is true** is narrower and worth acting on separately: for a LINKED bond both
are in the feed — `maturityDate` and `paymentSchedule` — so asking a person for them
is the same redundancy [`D119`](D119.md) removed for the coupon. The picker already
shows "matures 24.03.2027" as a hint and never writes it.

**`payoutSchedule` cannot simply be bound to `yieldType`.** All 32 live bonds pay
semiannually, but the enum's `maturity` is a zero-coupon bond — a real instrument,
merely none listed today — and for funds the feed publishes no schedule at all, so
there is nothing to derive from. **But the default pairing is wrong**:
`assetFormDefaults` opens a new asset as `fixed_coupon` + `maturity`, while both
seed bonds and every live one are `semiannual`. A derived default — from the feed's
own gaps when linked — is the honest fix, not a binding.
