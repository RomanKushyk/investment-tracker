## D111 — A public holiday is not a calendar class here (2026-08-31)

W3 asks, among six questions, "how does a public holiday read — same as a weekend,
or different?". Reading the window on 2026-08-31 the owner supplied the fact that
settles it: **public holidays are suspended in Ukraine under martial law.**

### What was measured

2026-08-24 was Independence Day. `nbu_fv` published normally:

| `as_of` | day | HTTP | entries | bytes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2026-08-21 | Fri | 200 | 185 | 22 103 |
| **2026-08-24** | **Mon** | **200** | **185** | **22 101** |
| 2026-08-25 | Tue | 200 | 185 | 22 099 |

Indistinguishable from its neighbours.

### Why the question is closed as moot rather than answered

For `inzhur` the window **cannot** answer it, and saying so is the honest outcome.
2026-08-24 was a **Monday** — the one weekday on which Inzhur fund NAV does not move
at all (measured: 0 of 5 funds on all three Mondays in the series). A holiday effect
and a Monday effect are inseparable in that observation, and any future holiday that
falls on a Sunday or Monday would be equally unreadable.

So the answer does not come from the archive. It comes from the fact that the class
does not currently exist.

### The rule

**Treat a public holiday as an ordinary calendar day** — for capture, for
completeness checks, and for alarm thresholds. Do not add a holiday calendar, and do
not special-case a date because it is a holiday.

### Revisit

**When martial law ends**, not on a date. Nothing watches for it — that is
[`D106`](D106.md)'s ruling and it applies here: the trigger is read by a person, and
this entry is where they will find what it changes. At that point the question
reopens for `nbu_fv` (does it stop publishing?) and stays hard for `inzhur` whenever
the holiday lands on a Sunday or Monday.
