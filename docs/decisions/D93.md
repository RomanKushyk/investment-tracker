## D93

**The ledger's width cap comes off: inside a grid track, the track is the
bound (2026-08-25)**

Owner instruction, on `/transactions`: the history card sits in D88's
`grid-cols-[1.6fr_1fr]`, and `max-w-[884px]` inside a bounded track no longer
protects anything — it opens a dead strip between the ledger and the form, and
the two columns stop reading as one grid. The cap comes off; the card fills its
track at every width.

**The cap had history, which is why this is an entry and not just a diff.** It
was removed once and deliberately restored ("`max-w-[884px]` IS BACK" stood in
the code), defended in two comments, pinned by `transactions-layout.test.ts`
and described in `navigation-map.md` — without a number here, the old comment's
reasoning would have argued it back in a third time. Every site that asserted
the cap is updated in the same change: both comments, the test,
`navigation-map.md` (the checkpoint and the route's Status cell) and
`PLAN-NOW.md`'s two cap lines.

**What the cap was defending, and why that lost.** On a very wide monitor the
track runs past 884 and a `justify-between` row stretches with it — label at
one end, amount and date at the other. That cost is real and is accepted: the
row-readability answer, if it is ever wanted, belongs to the ROW (a content
max-width inside the card, a different row grammar), not to a card that
un-fills its own track. The precedents already agreed: `/` dropped its own 884
for the 144 px reflow it hid (see `navigation-map.md` § `/`), and `/payouts`'
wide track never carried a cap — this was the last one.

**Untouched on purpose** *(this half superseded by D94 the same day — shown
the price below, the owner made the call this paragraph left open)*: the
form's `max-w-[560px]` — and its exemption is
priced honestly, not waved through. The 560 is a CONTENT measure, not a track
job: a form stretched wide reads as a settings page, and that is as true
beside the ledger as in the collapsed column below `lg`. It does bind in-track
on wide monitors — above a ~1800 viewport the `1fr` track outgrows 560, so the
form stops short of its track (~48 px at 1920, ~294 px at 2560). That strip is
accepted here because the cap's reason survives where the ledger's did not; if
it ever offends, the moves are `max-lg:max-w-[560px]` or carrying the measure
inside the card — an owner call this entry deliberately does not make. The
test pins both directions — the form cap present, the ledger card's own class
string free of `max-w-`.
