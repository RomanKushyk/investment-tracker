## D68 — `muted` is re-derived against the surface it is worst on, and `label` is retired into it

**2026-08-17.** Design session for `docs/plans/FOLLOW-UPS.md` item 15. Reference:
`design/extensions/muted-legibility.dc.html`. Implementation is a separate task,
gated on that reference the way G7 gates every other.

**`--color-muted` becomes `#696865` in the light theme. `--color-label` is
deleted, and its thirteen call sites take `text-muted`.** Dark is untouched.

**THE DEFECT IS LIGHT-THEME ONLY, and that is the first thing the measurement
settled.** Phase 5 measured the dark sheet — 23 checks, 0 failures — and it
holds: across nine routes every `muted` in dark reads **5.56 to 6.53** and every
`label` **6.50 to 7.06**. The LIGHT palette came from the immutable master
reference and was never measured against WCAG 1.4.3. Measured on the rendered app,
**258 elements across ten routes**:

| backdrop | ratio | elements |
|---|---|---|
| `panel` #eceae7 | **2.88 : 1** | 9 |
| `page` #f6f5f3 | **3.17 : 1** | 34 |
| `card` #ffffff | **3.46 : 1** | 215 |

**Every one fails, and there is no exemption to hide behind.** The bar is 4.5 : 1
rather than 3 : 1 because all 258 render between 9.5 px and 13 px at weight 400 —
not one reaches WCAG's "large text" (≥ 24 px, or ≥ 18.66 px bold).

**THE SECOND FINDING DECIDED THE SHAPE OF THE FIX.** `--color-label` #6f6e6a sat
beside `muted`, minted for "Transaction panel field labels". It measures **4.25 on
panel**, 4.68 on page, 5.10 on card — so it fails too, on the same surface and
only there. Solve both against the worst backdrop and they land on the same
luminance. **Two tokens that must hold the same value to be legible are not two
steps in a scale; they are one step drawn twice.** Darkening `muted` alone would
have put `label` LIGHTER than it — inverting the very relationship the pair
existed to express — and keeping two steps would have meant minting a third,
darker value for `label` that buys nothing legible. So the palette gets *smaller*.

**Why `#696865` and not the value already in the palette.** The binding surface is
`panel`, L 0.82448, so 4.5 : 1 needs the text at L ≤ **0.14433**:

| candidate | L | panel | verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| `#8b8a86` today | 0.25387 | 2.88 | fails on all three |
| `#6f6e6a` today's `label` | 0.15572 | 4.25 | fails on `panel` |
| `#6b6a67` | 0.14413 | **4.50** | lands exactly ON the bar |
| **`#696865`** | 0.13843 | **4.64** | 0.14 of headroom |

`#6b6a67` was rejected for a reason worth keeping: 4.50 is not a pass, it *is* the
bar to the last digit the check reports, and any future panel tint or a
sub-pixel-rendered glyph spends it. `#696865` also stays on the palette's warm
axis — R 105 > G 104 > B 101, the same ordering as #8b8a86 (139/138/134) and
#6f6e6a (111/110/106). No neutral grey is introduced; no hue is minted.

**THE COST, STATED RATHER THAN GLOSSED.** The step from `ink` narrows from
**4.36 : 1 to 2.71 : 1** — `muted` becomes a quieter `ink` rather than a distant
grey. That is the real price. It is still plainly a second rank, and the
alternative is a hierarchy that a reader with ordinary low-contrast difficulty
cannot resolve at all. **Hierarchy that depends on being hard to read is not
hierarchy.**

**Three non-text consumers ride along, all upward.** The `Scroller` thumb while
dragging (`bg-muted`) goes 3.46 → **5.57** on card, so **D65's recorded figure
changes while its argument does not** — the contrast-passing value still arrives
at the moment the thumb is in use. The quote row's ghost-input hover border and
the charts' 10 px axis tick labels improve with it; those tick labels are text and
were failing 1.4.3 along with everything else.

**What this does not touch.** `ink`, `faint`, every tint family, the four asset
hues, and the whole dark sheet. `faint` stays decorative-only and stays where it
is — 1.4.11 governs it and D65 already argues its resting value deliberately. The
asset hues stay too: A8 recorded that the light theme leaves them below even 3 : 1
and that it is out of scope, and that stands, because they are never text.

**This is a deliberate divergence from the master reference**, which prints
`muted #8b8a86`. It is the second such divergence and it is recorded rather than
silent — D54 moved the fonts away from the reference for the same class of reason
(the drawn choice cannot do the job the app needs). D14 gives a merged drawing the
visual dispute; the master reference is immutable and stays as drawn.
