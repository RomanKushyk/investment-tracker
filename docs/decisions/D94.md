## D94

**The form cap is scoped to the stacked column — the call D93 left to the
owner, made the same day (2026-08-25)**

D93 removed the ledger's width cap and kept the form's `max-w-[560px]`
unconditional, pricing the asymmetry rather than hiding it: above a ~1800
viewport the `1fr` track outgrows 560 and the form un-fills its own track
(~48 px at 1920, ~294 px at 2560). It named the moves and declined to choose —
"an owner call this entry deliberately does not make". Shown the price, the
owner chose the first move: **`max-lg:max-w-[560px]`**.

**The rule is now uniform:** beside the ledger, at any width, the TRACK is the
bound for both cards — D93's principle with no exception left. The 560 content
measure survives where it was always doing its real work: the stacked single
column below `lg`, where no track bounds anything and a form stretched to the
row's full width reads as a settings page rather than an entry.

**Supersedes the form half of D93's "Untouched on purpose" paragraph**
(annotated there in place). The test pins each card's exact width-token list —
`max-lg:max-w-[560px]` is the form card's only width token (cap, `w-[…]` or
`basis-[…]`, under any variant; `min-w-*` floors stay legal) and the ledger
card has none — with the collapse prefix read from `/payouts`' own grid row
rather than restated. The side-block comment that always said "capped at 560
when the grid collapses" is now literally the code.
