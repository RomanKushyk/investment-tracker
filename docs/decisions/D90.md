## D90

**What a week of real DPU corrected in D64, and the question it left open
against D48 (2026-08-25)**

W2's measurement — taken 2026-08-17, full working in `infra/README.md` § "W2 — a
week of real DPU" — falsified two figures inside **D64** and left one question
standing against **D48**. Recorded here, on the day the W2 gate row was finally
closed, because the correction had been living only in a plan file and a README
while the decision the numbers came from still read as true.

**Superseded in D64: the guard costs ~73 DPU a month, not ~6.** D64 reasoned that
`alreadySettled`'s indexed lookup is "a minimum-size read". It is not — the
lookup reads **117 KiB**, tens of times the 2 KiB minimum the estimate assumed,
so five no-op firings a day cost ~2.4 DPU/day rather than ~0.2. Twelve times the
estimate, and still free in every sense that matters.

**Superseded in D64: the workload uses ~1.6% of the DPU allowance, not 0.3%.**
~1,620 DPU/month measured against the 100,000 always-free tier — five times the
~325 the cost spec projected.

**D64's RULING is untouched.** The six-firing retry schedule still costs nothing
measurable, which is what D64 decided. What was wrong was a supporting estimate,
not the conclusion it supported, and that distinction is why this entry
supersedes two numbers rather than reopening a decision.

**Left open against D48, and this is the half worth watching.** D48 concluded the
missing index was the fix, and that the two operational queries scan zero
filtered rows, one of them as an Index Only Scan. W2 then measured a full capture
reading **34.2 MiB against 34.9 MiB of total cluster storage** — it reads
approximately everything, which no index-only path explains. This is *not* yet a
finding against D48: two consecutive full captures were flat at 69.06 and 69.04
DPU, and a scan over a growing archive would not stay flat. **W6 (2026-09-10)
settles it.** If the number grows with the archive, the year-20 projection of
~6,506 DPU/month is wrong and the index is not covering the path the capture
actually takes; if it stays flat, D48 stands entirely.

**Why an entry and not an edit.** D64 is cited by bare number from `infra/`, and
rewriting its paragraph would erase both that an estimate was made and how far
off it was. "The estimate assumed a minimum-size read and the read was 57× that"
is the reusable lesson — the same reason D43's wrong diagnosis is kept beside its
replacement rather than deleted.
