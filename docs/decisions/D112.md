## D112 — Units are derived from the ledger (2026-08-31)

[Issue #31](https://github.com/RomanKushyk/investment-tracker/issues/31): "Отримані
котирування суттєво не співпадають з тими які б мали бути. Можливо це привязано то
кількості одиниць введених через транзакції, бо вводиться лише при першій покупці,
наступні купівлі приймають лише суму грн."

The reporter's hypothesis was right, and the cause was one step worse than it reads.

### What was measured

A position linked at 5 000 units that later reinvests ₴11 138.90 of dividends,
priced at the feed's own sell price (11.1389) so the arithmetic needs no history:

| | ₴ |
|---|---|
| what the fetch offered | 55 694.50 |
| what the position was worth | 66 833.40 |
| **missing** | **11 138.90 — 16.7% of the position** |

### The root cause, traced

| where | what it held |
|---|---|
| `Asset.inzhur.units` | **one hand-typed number**, written only by the asset form |
| `Transaction` | `{ id, date, type, assetId, amount, source }` — **no units field** |
| `Snapshot.quotes[id]` | a **position value in ₴**, not a per-unit price |
| `matchAssets` | `positionValue(link.units, sellUAH)` — the app's **only** units × price arithmetic |

The step worse: the correction was not computable from what was stored. Turning a
past ₴ purchase into units needs the per-unit price on that date, and the app kept
no per-unit price anywhere — the seed even models a reinvestment as a *bump added to
the position value* (`seed.ts` PATHS). A data-model gap, not an arithmetic slip.

### Why this is not new design

W7 had already decided it. `infra/schema/user.ts` gives `transaction` a `quantity`
and a `unit_price`, gives `asset` **no units column at all**, and writes the model
out in a comment: `value(a, D) = units(a, D) × coalesce(user_price(a, D),
archive(a, D))`. `w7-migration-translations.md` §4 says the same in words — "units
become a derivation rather than a stored total".

So the local model moves **forward to the target**, and a third shape is not
invented. `derive.ts`'s `unitsByAsset` is `Σ quantity deltas`; `buy`/`reinvest` add,
`sell`/`redemption` remove.

### What stays optional, and why it must

`Transaction.quantity` and `unitPrice` are optional in the type, in
`transactionSchema`, in the JSON backup row and as empty CSV cells. Every row
recorded before this carries neither, and §4 says those counts are **unrecoverable**.
Requiring them would reject the app's own history. `transactionSchema` enforces only
the one-way rule W7's `transaction_quantity_absent_ck` enforces: a row that moves no
position must not carry units; a position-moving row may lack them.

### The fallback is deliberate, and it is visible

`Asset.inzhur.units` is **kept**, and `matchAssets` uses it for any asset the ledger
cannot answer for. Two reasons, and the second is the load-bearing one:

1. Until a position's transactions carry quantities, the hand-typed total is
   genuinely the best number available.
2. **It is the only seed for the backfill.** Removing it before the ledger carries
   quantities would destroy the number W7's migration needs — §4 again.

`InzhurMatch.unitsFrom` reports `'ledger'` or `'link'` so the two are never
presented as one number. Presence decides, never truthiness: a fully sold position
sums to 0, and `??` on a falsy 0 would value a closed holding at its old size.

**Backfill route, owner's ruling 2026-08-31: by hand.** The units field stays in the
asset form until the owner has entered quantities on the real transactions; removing
it is a separate, later change.

### What is explicitly NOT in this decision

**Per-unit quotes.** `Snapshot.quotes[id]` stays a position value. Converting it
means rewriting the seed, and that was tried as A52 and withdrawn: the row count
`4/174/18` and `Deposited 143 176 ₴` are pinned checkpoints, so no row moves for
free. The seed rewrite and the `derive.ts` change land together at W7, and they wait
on [`PLAN-OPEN`](../plans/PLAN-OPEN.md) **O31**. Owner's ruling, 2026-08-31: defer.

### Also fixed by the same change

`couponPrefill` sized a coupon from the same stale total, and a coupon is paid per
unit held — so it did not merely misprice a display, it prefilled the **amount of a
transaction about to be recorded**. It now takes the ledger's count as of the
coupon's own date.
