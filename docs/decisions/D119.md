## D119 — The coupon rate replaces the coupon amount (2026-08-31)

The owner's framing was right: *«кожна облігація завжди має фіксовану суму купона
(відсоток)»*. The research confirmed it and made the derivation exact.

## What was measured

One public `GET /_api/assets`, 2026-08-31 — 37 entries, 32 bonds. Every figure is
all 32. Detail and reproduction: [`OVDP-COUPON-STRUCTURE.md`](../reference/OVDP-COUPON-STRUCTURE.md).

| property | measured |
|---|---|
| principal at maturity | **₴1000 per unit** |
| distinct coupon values within one bond | **exactly 1** |
| gap between coupon dates | **182 days** (90 of 92 gaps) |
| coupon dates on a Wednesday | **123 of 124** |

**One value per bond is the load-bearing observation.** It proves the convention is
a fixed amount per period, not ACT/365 — a day-count coupon would differ between a
182-day and a 181-day period, and none does.

```
annual coupon rate % = perUnitCoupon × 2 / 1000 × 100 = perUnitCoupon ÷ 5
```

Range 9.79 %…18.50 %, 25 distinct values, every one exact to two decimals.

## `returnRates` is the YTM, and conflating them would be wrong twice over

| ISIN | coupon rate | `returnRates.sell` | gap |
|---|---|---|---|
| UA4000229264 | 17.80 % | 13.50 % | **+4.30 pp** |
| UA4000238992 | 16.16 % | 16.75 % | **−0.59 pp** |

The sign changes with the discount or premium. The coupon rate is **published
nowhere in the feed** — deriving it from the schedule is the only way to have it.

## What changed

`Asset.couponRatePct` is what the form asks for. `couponPerPayment(asset, units)`
computes the ₴:

```
ratePct / 100 / paymentsPerYear × OVDP_FACE_UAH × units
```

**The rate is the fixed property; the amount is not.** A coupon rate is set at
issuance and never moves; the ₴ a coupon pays moves every time the holding does —
which is exactly why the stored amount went stale on every purchase, the defect
[#31](https://github.com/RomanKushyk/investment-tracker/issues/31) reported for
`Asset.inzhur.units`.

Rewired onto it: the ghost accrual rate, the coupon-due card, `/overview`'s payout
projection, `/seasonality`'s bars and bond ranking, and `/attributes`' display.

## It agrees with the provider by construction

`ratePct / 100 × 1000 / 2 = ratePct × 5`, and `ratePct × 5` **is** the per-unit
coupon the feed publishes. So a linked bond valued from the rate and one valued from
`paymentSchedule` cannot disagree.

That closes a live inconsistency this work found: the coupon card prefilled
`feed × units` while `accrualSuggestion` and `couponProjection` read the hand-typed
absolute — and `accrualSuggestion` took the *period* from the feed while ignoring
its *amount*. Two bases for one coupon, on one screen.

## `couponAmount` stays, as legacy

Optional, never written again, read only when the rate cannot answer. The seed's two
bonds are why: they carry a hand-typed whole-position figure and their ledgers hold
no quantities to scale a rate by. Every D5-pinned figure is therefore unchanged —
the suite confirms it.

## `expectedPct` is NOT the coupon rate, and is not folded in

The owner proposed that "Очікувано, %" is redundant for a bond because it cannot
differ from the coupon rate. **The measurement says otherwise.** On a bond
`expectedPct` renders as *YTM at purchase* — the yield implied by the price **this
holder paid**, so the same bond bought on two dates has two values, while its coupon
rate has one for life. The table above is that gap, measured.

The instinct has a better form, recorded here rather than acted on: YTM at purchase
is **derivable** — price paid is `unitPrice` on the buy transaction (#31 added it),
the schedule comes from the feed, and `dcf.ts` already implements the bisection
inverse as `impliedYield`. So the field could become derived rather than asked for.
That is a separate change and is **not** made here.

## The assumption, stated

`OVDP_FACE_UAH = 1000` is the UAH OVDP nominal — true for all 32 measured, false for
a USD/EUR-denominated bond. A constant with the reference doc attached, not a field;
the tell that it has stopped holding is a principal row that is not 100000 kopecks.
