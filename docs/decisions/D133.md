## D133 — The seed's pinned figures may move (2026-09-02)

`PLAN-OPEN.md` O31 has been open since 2026-08-26, raised by A52's withdrawal.
It is W7's last unanswered gate: the phase's Seed bullet cannot be executed
until this is ruled.

### The constraint, stated as arithmetic

[`D34`](D34.md) requires the seed to gain `withdrawal` rows and separate `tax`
rows, so that the account sum reconciles **by construction** rather than through
the exclusion rules `derive.ts` carries today. D34 also claims that *"every
D5-pinned figure and every `navigation-map.md` checkpoint stays valid; only the
fixture beneath them changes."*

**That claim is false, and A52 is where it was measured.** `freeCashFromLedger`
(`src/core/derive.ts`) is

```
Σ deposit − Σ buy − Σ withdrawal
```

which today is `143 176,37 − 143 168,62 = 7,75`. So:

- Any `withdrawal` of X lowers free cash by X.
- Restoring free cash needs a `deposit` of X, which moves
  **`Deposited ₴143 176,37`** — pinned by D5#6 and by checkpoint 7.
- The added row moves the **transaction count of 18** either way — pinned by
  D10, D24 and checkpoint 7.

**At least two of the three must give.** There is one arrangement that holds
every value — pair each withdrawal with a `sell` or `redemption` of the same
amount, since both add to free cash — and it is not a free lunch: it changes
the holdings, so portfolio value, units and XIRR move instead. The pins simply
trade places.

### The ruling

**All three may move.** The seed is written as the ledger model requires, and
the checkpoints are **re-derived from the new seed** rather than defended.

Superseded **as figures, not as rulings**: D10's
`4 assets / 174 snapshots / 18 transactions`, D24's dataset line, D5 #6's
`Deposited ₴143,176`, **and D5 #1's `₴149,016.36`** — that one is quotes + cash,
so releasing free cash releases it too, and leaving it out would have looked
like a judgement rather than the oversight it was.

Every argument those entries make is untouched — what expires is a figure taken
against a fixture that will not exist.

### What it actually costs, corrected

O31 reads as though moving `4/174/18` means chasing it through the
documentation. **The documentation half is nearly free; the CODE half is not,
and an earlier draft of this entry said "exactly one live literal" and was
plainly wrong.** What is actually there:

| Where the figures appear | What it costs |
|---|---|
| `navigation-map.md` checkpoint 7, `docs/design-briefs/phase-7/s3-s4.md` | **fact fences** — `pnpm facts` rewrites them from `SEED_ASSETS`, `buildSeedSnapshots()` and `SEED_TRANSACTIONS` |
| checkpoint 7's `Added +4 / +174 / +18`, **and its `149 016 ₴`, `Deposited 143 176 ₴`, `Free cash 7,75 ₴`** | hand-written literals in the one checkpoint, all of them figures this entry releases |
| **`src/facts/registry.test.ts`, `src/lib/seed.test.ts`, `src/lib/repository.test.ts`** | **live code asserting `4` / `174` / `18` directly.** `registry.test.ts`'s own comment says a change there "needs a decision, not a green test" — this is that decision |
| **198 `it()` blocks across 12 files** (measured 2026-08-26, recorded in W7's Seed bullet) | the real size of the rewrite |
| `D10`, `D24`, `D112`, and the plans discussing O31 | decisions and prose *about* the pin — superseded or historical, never edited |

**So this ruling makes the seed rewrite POSSIBLE, not cheap.** The
documentation follows the seed by itself; the test suite does not, and the
plan's own 198-block figure is the number to size the work by. Anyone reading
this entry for an estimate should read that bullet, not this table.

**And two commitments already made mean the checkpoints were moving anyway.**
[`D128`](D128.md) rules that W7 seeds fresh demo data and migrates nothing, and
W7's own scope already requires a full `navigation-map.md` re-baseline in the
same phase. This ruling does not create that work; it removes the reason to
fight it.

### What does NOT move

**The checkpoints' purpose.** They exist so that a round trip cannot silently
change a figure, and checkpoint 7 states the rule itself: *"if a round-trip
moves a pinned number the serializer is wrong, never the checkpoint."* That
survives intact and re-applies to whatever the new figures turn out to be. A
checkpoint that is re-derived once, deliberately, at a phase that rewrites the
fixture underneath it, is not the same thing as a checkpoint that drifts.

**D34's ruling.** Its *reasoning* — that the seed must reconcile the way real
data does, and that a purpose-built minimal fixture was the wrong answer — is
re-affirmed. Only its promise that no figure would move is corrected, and it was
already contradicted by measurement before this entry.

### Consequence

**W7's Seed bullet is executable.** It was the phase's one item blocked on a
question rather than on work, and A52 exists in the archive as the attempt that
proved why. The seed rewrite and the `derive.ts` change still land together —
that half of A52's finding stands — and they land inside W7.
