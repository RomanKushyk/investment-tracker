## D105 — Plans first, then issues one at a time (2026-08-28)

Owner's ruling, 2026-08-28, given in the session that also produced
[`D104`](D104.md), and split out of it when a draft of that entry tripped the
200-line diagnostic and its question — does this file hold a second purpose? —
answered yes. The cap reports, it does not compel (D98); the split was the
answer to its question, not obedience to a limit. The rule reached `docs/plans/README.md` and this entry in
the same change — nothing about it predates them in `git`. D104 is about
which GitHub surfaces the repository uses; this is about the order work is taken
in.

### The ruling

**Finish the live plans as they stand, then work issues one at a time.**

**The RULE is durable; the list below is a snapshot of 2026-08-28.** Three kinds
of row are out: the four far-gated W items named below (W9–W12), a task an
index marks denied or withdrawn, and a question it marks open by design or
cheap to leave. Everything else live is in. **The three indexes are the live authority** —
this entry is never rewritten, so its IDs will name closed work soon enough, and
a reader who takes them as a to-do list is reading a dated record as a plan.

What "finished" covered on the day:

- **Plan A** — A53, A54, A46. (A11 is denied and A52 withdrawn; both are kept
  for their reasons, not their work.)
- **Plan C** — the five questions waiting on a ruling: **O27, O28, O31, O32,
  O33**. Not the nine `PLAN-OPEN.md` counts: O34 and O35 closed on 2026-08-28
  (D101, D102), and O29/O30 cost nothing to leave. O9, O10, O11 and O5's
  remainder are open BY DESIGN and are not among the nine at all.
- **Plan B** — **every live W item except W9–W12**: W3–W8, **W13, W14**, W15,
  W16, W17. That includes **Phase 6's chart analytics (W13)** and **Phase 7's DB
  browser (W14)**, both gated on W7.

**"All the current plans" is meant literally, and the owner said so when asked.**
A first draft of this entry excluded W13 and W14 on the reasoning that two large
downstream items would park the inbox indefinitely, and flagged the reading as
the entry's own rather than the owner's. Put to him directly on 2026-08-28, the
answer was **literally all the plans**. The exclusion is withdrawn; the flag did
its job, which is the argument for writing such a reading down rather than
letting it pass as the ruling.

**The excluded set is FOUR NAMED ITEMS: W9, W10, W11, W12.** Their gates were
2027-01-01, 2027-03-24, 2027-07-29 and 2028-09-27 — four months to two years
beyond this ruling. **Naming them rather than stating a cut-off is deliberate:**
a rule phrased as "gates in 2027 or later" excludes every remaining W item the
moment 2027 arrives, and a rule phrased as "a year or more away" would have
included W9 on the day it was written. A dated entry that is never rewritten
cannot carry a moving threshold. For a W item added later, the reason is the
test: is this work that can be finished, or a calendar event to be waited out?

An earlier draft said they were excluded because their gates are *dates no work
brings forward* — equally true of W3 (2026-09-02), W6 (2026-09-10) and W5
(2026-09-24, and hard), all three of which are IN. Distance was the difference,
not kind.

**Then issues, one at a time**, each on its own branch under D73, closed by
`Closes #N` in the squash-merge. `bug` is the one class allowed to preempt the
order — that is what the `Bugs` saved view is for (D108).

### Why, and what it narrows

The owner's reason was that working an issue directly **does not grow a task
body in `docs/plans`**. The inbox was moved out of the repository by D103
precisely because it was accumulating there; routing all 27 back in as plan
tasks would undo that in a slower way.

**This narrows D103, and says so rather than contradicting it quietly.** D103's
grooming clause reads: *"An issue becomes work by getting a Status row in the
right index and its body in the matching section file, in one commit."* That
path is not withdrawn — an issue big enough to need a plan body, or one that
turns out to be gated on something, still takes it. **It is no longer the
default.** The default is: branch, do the work, `Closes #N`, nothing added to
`docs/plans`.

D103 itself was left unedited on the owner's instruction, which is also what
`RULES.md` requires: a decision is narrowed by a later entry, never rewritten.

### What this does not decide

- **Nothing about triage.** The milestone still means "read, and decided it
  waits", set by hand; D108 records why no workflow sets it.
- **No ordering among the issues themselves.** They sit in one milestone with
  no rank. If an order is ever wanted, the Project board is where it goes — it
  holds issues only, so a rank there costs nothing in `git`.
