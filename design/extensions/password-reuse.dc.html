T6 · Refused

  • A second line under the rule. GOV.UK adds its message “after the question text and hint text”, and Amplify UI’s ForceNewPassword puts RemoteErrorMessage between the fields and the button. Here it moves the button, unless an empty line is reserved on every visit for a refusal most visits never meet: two lines in English at 360.
  • The step’s slot under the button. The sentence is about the field’s value and would sit away from it, and no other sentence in that slot carries a glyph.
  • Cognito’s message verbatim. “Password has previously been used” says neither which password nor what to do; the owner ruled a sentence of the app’s own.
  • Comparing the two passwords in the page. The pool’s history is the one rule (owner ruling at triage); a second copy in the page could drift from it.
  • Clearing the field. GOV.UK: “Do not clear any form fields when showing the Error message component”. The field keeps what was typed, as T5’s refusal does.
