The field edge, re-derived

Mints --color-field-border and re-values --color-pos-border. Every figure is measured on the rendered app through the chrome-devtools MCP at 1440×900, dpr 1, transitions disabled, each theme reached by reload.

Supersedes the field-edge treatment of all ten merged drawings that draw one — the master reference, daily-quotes-live, appearance-language, asset-form, where-things-live, settings, automation, screen-density, period-and-analytics and metrics-exposure. None of the ten is edited.

A field's stroke is read against its own fill and against the plane behind it. The second is what the survey turned up: on /transactions six fields sit on card inside a panel, so the stroke is read against #eceae7. THE CHECKBOXES ARE NOT IN THIS TABLE, AND THAT IS A MEASUREMENT RATHER THAN AN OVERSIGHT. CouponDueCard.tsx:200 and ImportDialog.tsx:249 read size-4 rounded-[5px] border-panel-border bg-page — but they carry no border-WIDTH utility, and Tailwind's preflight sets border: 0 solid, so the token paints nothing: rendered, they report border-top-width: 0px. They are also native <input type="checkbox"> with no appearance-none anywhere in src, so at appearance: auto the user agent draws the control and discards the author's border, background and radius — adding a border class changes nothing, which was tested rather than assumed. Giving them a real edge means appearance-none and a hand-drawn tick: new UI, and its own brief.

T4 · The six rulings

1 · The token

--color-field-border: #84827d light, #747169 dark. The field idle edge points at it, replacing hairline. It is a control-boundary rank, not a text rank — which is why it is minted rather than folded into faint (T2). Palette 42 → 43.

2 · The saved edge

--color-pos-border: #6f8567 light, #5c7f53 dark. It moves because leaving it inverts the pair against the repaired idle edge. pos is refused at 4.33 / 6.55 — outside the 1.10x band from the other side, and already the figure green.

3 · Hover goes to ink

All ten field hover sites, plus the ghost input and the "Use suggested" pill, whose hover:border-muted would measure 1.45 against the new idle edge. muted is refused on that measurement — smaller than the weakest hover shipping today (1.55, the dropzone). ink steps 3.93 / 4.06, gentler than the 12.19 three sites already ship. QuoteRow's idle, filled and unreadable branches carry no hover today and gain none: that is behaviour, not colour.

4 · Dashed: controls move, containers do not

Two of the five dashed faint elements are CONTROLS and take the new token, dashed — the ghost input and the accept pill. Three are CONTAINERS and keep faint: the coupon-due card, the import diff panel and the quick-create sub-form. A panel that HOLDS a proposal is not itself proposing. So daily-quotes-live's "one stroke everywhere" narrows to one stroke on every dashed CONTROL, recorded here rather than discovered later. The pill has a second branch and it keeps warn: QuoteRow.tsx:82 swaps stroke and label to warn when the fetched quote is stale, and measured that reads 4.05 / 4.86 / 4.46 in light and 6.90 / 7.50 / 8.11 in dark — already above the bar on every surface, so it does not move. It carries no border hover today and gains none.

5 · Select's borderColor prop

It declares 'hairline' | 'faint' and TransactionPanel.tsx:785 passes faint for the new-asset trigger. Both arms now resolve to the same edge, so the prop, its branch and that one ternary go. isNewAsset itself STAYS — it gates the quick-create sub-form at :617, :819 and :1072 and transaction-form-reset.test.ts:169 pins its declaration. The state it encoded was the LOUDER of the two arms — faint is 1.77 on panel against hairline's 1.03 in light, 3.03 against 1.10 in dark — so the new-asset trigger was drawn with a heavier edge, not a fainter one. Either way it is a stroke-weight cue for "proposed", which is the device this ruling replaces with dash; if the state is wanted it returns dashed, in its own task.

6 · Fills stay where they are

Two merged sheets disagree — appearance-language line 828 says page inside a card, daily-quotes-live draws #fff in #fff — and the app ships both: seven recipes hard bg-page, two hard bg-card, two parameterised. This sheet does not close it. It need not: a fill step measures 1.09 light / 1.08 dark, so the stroke was always the carrier.

T5 · What this costs, and what it deliberately leaves alone

The cost is one palette value and one narrowed rule. The palette goes 42 → 43, and in dark the new token sits 1.06 : 1 from faint — visually the same stroke, for now, on two roles that must be able to part. In light they are already 1.81 : 1 apart. The narrowed rule is T4-4: dash stays one stroke across CONTROLS, not across containers.

What it buys is everything the alternative could not leave alone. faint keeps its value, so the thirteen text-faint sites, the Scroller thumb and its argued sub-3 : 1 rest, the native scrollbar, the chart's zero-value stub, the three dashed containers, the dropzone's hover, and every faint figure written into index.css and Scroller.tsx are untouched and need no re-measurement. Three shipped comments DO go stale and are named rather than left: AssetForm.tsx:67 ("hover border → faint"), Select.tsx:8 ("hairline border") and Select.tsx:9, which documents the prop T4-5 deletes. Three lines, against the dozen-plus the alternative would have invalidated across index.css and Scroller.tsx.
