The terms of use: /terms, the acceptance line on /apply, and the link beside «Конфіденційність»

Brief: #514, as a comment on the issue. Implementing task: #515. Every frame is the running app with the new surfaces cloned from the live ones beside them and serialized from computed styles, so a value here is a value the app already renders.

T1 · /terms, for every reader

/privacy’s page, holding the terms. A page with no portfolio behind it mounts neither app shell (Two shells, one breakpoint), so a signed-in reader sees the same page; nothing waits on the session. The card at max-width: 680px, «До Quirenote» above the title, «Останнє оновлення», two intro paragraphs, «Зміст» and twelve numbered sections in /privacy’s type: body 13/19,5 ink, titles 15/22,5, each section 28 under the last. Under the card the two links stand on one line, «Конфіденційність · Умови», and «Умови» carries aria-current="page" here. Under a refused caller’s answer both open a new tab, as the privacy link does there (SignedOutShell’s notice="tab").

T2 · /apply: the acceptance line above the button, the first layer under it

Directly above «Подати заявку»: one sentence that names the button and links the terms. «Натискаючи «Подати заявку», ви приймаєте Умови користування.», 12/18 muted, the link accent and underlined, opening /terms in a new tab as the first layer’s does, its name ending «(відкривається в новій вкладці)». It stands 12 under the widget and 8 over the button, so it reads with the button; nothing sits between them. The first layer stays as #497 draws it, 12 under the button. A step alert enters under the button, above the first layer, and the button does not move. The two texts are two paragraphs, the button between them.

T3 · The panel and the drawer: two lines

«Конфіденційність · Умови» over the version. One line would need 231,72 at 11 px against the panel’s 203, so the links take a line of their own and the version a second, 4 under it. The band grows 18,25 upward, a fixed difference no state moves.

T4 · /profile: the terms row

A third row in «Ваші дані», under a divider after «Як ми обробляємо дані»: «Умови користування», its helper, and an outline «Умови» with a chevron to /terms, the row recipe the privacy row uses.
