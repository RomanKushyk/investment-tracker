The Ukrainian thousands separator is U+00A0, never a plain space — a plain space lets a figure wrap mid-number. Every Ukrainian figure in this file carries a real U+00A0 in the markup, so it can be copied rather than retyped.
Open, and worth deciding with A10: the gap before % and before ₴/$ is a plain space here, because the brief pins U+00A0 for thousands only. It is exposed to the same defect — a lone ₴ or % can wrap to the next line — so it probably wants U+00A0 too.
Ukrainian keeps a space before % (ДСТУ); English does not.
English dates are 12 Aug 2026, deliberately not 12/08/2026 — a slashed form is ambiguous between British and American reading.
The minus sign is U+2212 (−) and the ellipsis U+2026 (…) in both languages — an existing rule a translation pass is very likely to break.
What does NOT change
The stored data. Every D5-pinned value. And the ₴/$ toggle's scope: headline KPIs and the sidebar only — tables stay in ₴ regardless of language, because that is a currency rule, not a locale rule.
Consequence, stated plainly: table figures now re-format in English too. That touches core/money.ts, its tests, and every checkpoint in navigation-map.md that quotes a formatted string. A10 carries that cost — keeping tables Ukrainian in English mode is exactly the exception the ruling forbids.

Surface 1 · the 360px problem the brief did not see
Measured — the horizontal control cannot fit at 360px
At a 360px viewport the shell is: 136px rail + main px-3 (12px each) + card p-[22px] → 156px of card interior.

Horizontal control widths, measured at 0.6em advance:
· Currency ₴ UAH / $ USD — 158px (already 2px over, today)
· Language EN Ukrainian / English — 216px
· Language UK Українська / English — 230px
· Theme EN Light / Dark / System — 256px
· Theme UK Світла / Темна / Системна — 299px

The brief's §6 says the control "drops to its own line, right-aligned" at 360px — but its own line is still only 156px wide, so dropping does not save it. Below sm the segmented controls stack vertically: same track, same thumb, same motion, axis flipped. Track radius 20 (a 999 track three rows tall reads as a lozenge), segments stay radius 999, thumb height:calc(33.333% − 6px) and translateY in place of translateX.

Why every control stacks here
156px of card interior against a 299px control. The label block's min-width floor is already capped with min(200px,100%) so the card never overflows; the control follows the same principle by changing axis rather than by shrinking type or truncating a label.

The ml-auto rule survives: on a wrapped row the control still hugs the right edge — a left-aligned control on a wrapped row reads as a mistake (a real review finding on the ₴/$ rate row). When the control is full-width, right-hugging is satisfied by definition.

FINDING 3 · the two sites where `bg-ink` + `text-white` breaks in dark
1 · Filled emphasis — primary Button, Switch checked track, DatePicker selected day. bg-ink is CORRECT in both themes (dark fill in light, light fill in dark); the bug is the paired text-white, a literal that does not invert. Fix: text-page. In light that is #f6f5f3 against today's #ffffff — a change of 0.4% luminance, invisible; in dark it is #141416 on #eceae7, 15.32:1.

2 · Inverted planes — KpiCard variant="dark" (Overview "Total capital", Payouts) and the Dialog overlay bg-ink/40. These must STAY dark in both themes: their sub-line uses pos-on-dark, which the sheet leaves unchanged at #b9cdb4 precisely because "it was always for a dark plane". Fix: bg-ink → bg-sidebar. In light sidebar is #26262a — identical to ink, so it is a pixel-for-pixel no-op there — and in dark it becomes #0f0f11, keeping white at 19.15:1 and pos-on-dark at 11.36:1. Same for the overlay: bg-sidebar/40.

Neither fix adds a token, branches a component, or touches the light theme. Both are pre-requisites for A9 — without them the Overview KPI card renders white-on-white the moment dark is switched on.
