Sign-out in the shell — the panel, the rail, the drawer, and a sign-out that fails

Drawn from brief #277 and its amendment. A signed-in user sees the address they signed in with and a sign-out one press away, the footer band's last control in all three shells, which now read in one order; a sign-out that fails leaves them signed in and says so. Ukrainian in every frame but one, as the app defaults.

T1 · The panel

The footer band's last two rows above the version, under Settings at the nav's own step, 3. First the address, at a pill's geometry — 36 tall, the profile glyph at 16 in sb-icon where a pill has its own, the address at 11 px in sb-item, cut with an ellipsis and whole in its title. It is information until the profile page (#192) makes it that page's link, so it draws no hover. Then «Вийти», a pill in Settings' recipe: the LogOut glyph at 16 in sb-icon, the label at 13,5 in sb-item, r9, no fill at rest. Hover takes the nav's idle-hover pair, sb-item-hover-bg and sb-item-hover on the label; focus is the app's one base ring; busy is aria-disabled at 70 % and never disabled, so focus stays on the pill for the retry, and the rows stay until the route changes. An ID token with no readable address shows «Ви увійшли».

The slot is held until the relay answers — the moment between the first paint and the answer, and after a load it never answered, until the shell's next ask is answered — so when the answer is signed in the rows fade into space already there and nothing above them moves. Signed out, and on a host with no auth environment, the slot closes and the band is today's. The band is taller by the two rows and their steps, so the nav above it starts to scroll at a taller viewport — T5. The grid's column is made explicit, minmax(0, 1fr) in the panel and the drawer alike: with the implicit auto column the app has today, an address set on one line widens the column to its own length, and on the first draw of this sheet a 37-character address stretched the whole band to 350,41. A failed sign-out raises the toast of T4 at the page's bottom right, away from the band.

T2 · The rail

One order in both states. The currency box moves above Settings, as the currency track sits above it in the panel, and the sign-out follows Settings: the rail's foot reads ₴, Settings, «Вийти», 6 apart — the band's own step. This supersedes the foot band parchment-sidebar.dc.html draws (:76, frames :392 and :424), which is not edited. The sign-out is the rail item's own recipe, the LogOut glyph at 18 in a 40 × 36 box at r9, held empty while the session is unknown as in the panel. Its name is «Вийти», set by aria-label; the tooltip shows the same words, 16 to the right, and describes the button rather than naming it. It is an action, never a route, so it takes no tint and no indicator. No address in the rail: 56 holds none. The rail's nav starts to scroll at a taller viewport, still short of 640 — T5.

T3 · The drawer

The panel's two rows in the drawer's band, at the step the nav takes below 768, 8: a pill's 44 hit area reaches 3,875 past the 36,25 it draws, and the address row between Settings and «Вийти» holds nothing to press — T5. The address has 185 across. The toast stands on the band. At the page's bottom it would cover «Вийти», so the drawer publishes its band's rendered height while it is open and the toaster's bottom offset adds it; in the failed frames the toast sits 14 above the band, over the nav, and the retry stays in sight. The English frame shows the longest sentence.

T4 · A sign-out that fails

Sonner's error toast in the app's own styling — card, ink, a 1 px field-border edge, r13, the popover shadow — with no timeout: it stays until it is closed or a retry succeeds, and a second failure replaces the first so that it is announced again. The sentence says what failed and what is still true, then the one action: «Не вдалося вийти — ви досі в системі. Спробуйте ще раз.» The control it names is the retry, pressable again the moment the toast appears.

The close button moves inside. Sonner draws it as a 20 px circle astride the toast's corner; here it is 28 at r7 (28 × 0,26 = 7,28) in the toast's trailing edge, named «Закрити сповіщення», so its 44 overlay below 768 stays on the toast; at the corner it would reach past the screen's left edge. The drawer lets the toast take its taps. Measured in the app at 360 × 740 with the drawer open: the toast was 336 × 73 at y 653–726 over a band at y 620,5–740; the modal left the toaster's live region exposed but set pointer-events: none on the body, and a tap at the close button's centre landed on the band beneath. The toaster takes pointer events back, and the drawer ignores a press inside it.

T7 · Refused, and not drawn

  • An account menu — a second press for one item; the menus it copies switch accounts, and there is one.
  • An avatar — only asset avatars and colour dots are round; the profile glyph is a stroke.
  • A glyph-only button beside the address — the owner ruled the sign-out a pill like its neighbours.
  • The address as a link now — there is no profile page to open until #192.
  • A confirmation before signing out — NN/g: "Do not use confirmation dialogs for routine actions".
  • The rows first in the band — they moved nothing without holding a slot, but the owner ruled the sign-out last.
  • The rows appearing unheld — last in a band that grows upward, they would push the tracks and Settings up under a resting pointer when the session answers.
  • A toast that times out — Carbon, Atlassian and the APG alert pattern all refuse an error that disappears by itself.
  • A sentence under the control — the rail has no room for one, and one mechanism serves all three shells.
  • The toast at the top of a phone screen — toasts sit at the bottom (navigation-map.md); it stands on the drawer's band instead.
  • Sonner's corner close button — a circle, named in English, whose 44 overlay would reach past the screen's left edge at 360.
