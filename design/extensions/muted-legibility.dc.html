T2 · Why #696865 and not the value already in the palette

The binding surface is panel, L 0.82448. For 4.5 : 1 the text must sit at L ≤ (0.82448 + 0.05) / 4.5 − 0.05 = 0.14433.

Candidate L panel page card Verdict
#8b8a86 today 0.25387 2.88 3.17 3.46 fails on all three
#6f6e6a today's label 0.15572 4.25 4.68 5.10 fails on panel — the same surface
#6b6a67 0.14413 4.50 4.96 5.41 lands exactly ON the bar — no headroom at all
#696865 chosen 0.13843 4.64 5.11 5.57 0.14 of headroom on the worst surface
The warm axis is preserved. #696865 is R 105 > G 104 > B 101 — the same ordering as #8b8a86 (139/138/134) and #6f6e6a (111/110/106). The palette has no neutral greys and this does not introduce one.

T3 · label is retired into muted

Two tokens that must hold the same luminance to be legible are one step drawn twice.

today — two steps, and the darker one is label
Сума, ₴  label #6f6e6a
Джерело коштів  muted #8b8a86
after — one step, one name
Сума, ₴  muted #696865
Джерело коштів  muted #696865
Why not keep both and darken only muted. Solved against panel, muted lands at L 0.1384 while label sits at 0.1557 — so label would become the LIGHTER of the pair, inverting the relationship it was created to express. Keeping two steps would mean minting a third, darker value for label, pushing it toward ink and buying nothing legible. 13 call sites take text-muted instead; the visible change at those sites is L 0.1557 → 0.1384, which is below the threshold of sight.

In dark the collapse is equally quiet: those sites go from 7.06 : 1 to 6.04 : 1, both far past the bar, and the dark values are untouched.

T4 · What does not move

the whole dark sheet

Measured across nine routes: muted 5.56–6.53, label 6.50–7.06. Phase 5 measured it and it holds. Nothing here touches it.

faint #b3b2ae

Stays. It is decorative and never body text — the resting scroll thumb, a disabled dash. 1.4.11 governs it, not 1.4.3, and D65 already argues the resting value deliberately.

the four asset hues

Stay. A8 recorded that the light theme leaves them below 3 : 1 and that it is out of scope; they are never text, so this decision has nothing to say about them.

Three non-text consumers ride along, all upward. The Scroller thumb while dragging (bg-muted) goes 3.46 → 5.57 on card, so D65's recorded figure changes and its argument does not — the passing value still arrives at the moment the thumb is in use. The quote row's ghost-input hover border and the charts' 10 px axis tick labels improve with them; the tick labels were failing 1.4.3 along with everything else, since they are text.
