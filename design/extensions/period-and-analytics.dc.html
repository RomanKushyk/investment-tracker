Why the clamp is a LINE and not a chip — and what the line actually costs, which is not nothing. The window line is present in every state, so re-colouring it adds no element; a chip appears, and it appears below a dates line that stays. But the first two drafts of this sheet went on to claim “zero shift at ≥ md”, and that was false — D-3 is now canonical for this and the arithmetic is there. In short:
computed the clamped line is 23 + 3 + 40 = 66 ch × 6,6 = 435,6 px. Let the cluster shrink-to-fit and that 435,6 sets its width, so the flex-wrap header row needs 161 + 12 + 435,6 = 608,6 — a content box ≥ 609, i.e. a viewport ≥ 925 (≥ 991 in English). Between 768 and 925 the cluster drops to its own line and the header goes 55 → 39 + 12 + 55 = 106: +51 px on the press that produced it, which is the exact defect this whole decision exists to avoid, in a ~160 px band nobody measured.
