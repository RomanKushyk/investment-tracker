The own account: the profile page, its ways in, and what GET /me carries

The signed-in user’s own page at /profile: the account, how it signs in, the data it holds, and a way to end every session. The sidebar’s address becomes its link, the rail gains an item between Settings and «Вийти», and the drawer’s address links as the panel’s does. Brief: #491, a comment on the issue. GET /me is #492; the page is #192; signing out everywhere is #494.

T1 · The page

Four cards in Settings’ column: Обліковий запис, Ваші дані, Сеанси, Вхід. The Sign-in card is last because its list is a read of its own: web.dev, Optimize CLS, «If you can’t reserve space for injected content, we recommend placing it later on the page». A fact is a key and its value, after GOV.UK’s summary list: the key in a column of its own (220 px here; GOV.UK takes 30 %) and the value beside it, so the eye does not cross a 1026 px card; at 360 the value stands under its key. The role is a word for both roles. The member-since date is the approval’s, dd.MM.yyyy. The account ID is mono with «Копіювати» beside it, under the card’s one divider. The Google row shows only while Google sign-in is on (T2). An account with no asset or transaction yet reads 0, 0 and «—», the dash an answer in the value’s own ink. «До налаштувань» is a link to /settings given buttonVariants({ variant: 'outline' }), as NotFound’s way home is a link given the primary one. A passkey is a card of its own, its name and its created date: Cognito returns no last use and no provider.

T2 · Passkeys, every state the list reaches

The list is the relay’s read, apart from GET /me, so it has states of its own while the account above it stays in hand. None: one sentence. Signed in with Google, the list cannot be read, so the card says what shows it, a sign-in with the password or a passkey; the frame is a linked account that has a password, the one whose list a Google sign-in hides. With Google sign-in off, the card has no Google row. Failed: one sentence and the house’s remedy, a reload, nothing retried in place; the account stays, so this is a second exception to Not found and failures, beside the archive panel’s (Carbon places inline notifications «near their related items»; Atlassian’s section message alerts users «to a particular section of the screen»). In flight: three 61,5 px blocks at the cards’ radius, a list held as three rows whatever its length (Not found and failures); T7 measures the held card. The card is the page’s last, so when the list lands nothing below it moves; scrolled to the page’s foot, a list shorter than three rows shortens the page, the scroll clamps, and what is in view moves down by the difference (139 for the user’s one passkey, from T7’s 425 and 286). Until GET /me lands the card stays held whatever the list has answered, a Google session included; the list’s answer shows when /me lands. A note that arrives after the page is announced (role=status), #192’s to wire.

T3 · The account ID, copied

«Копіювати» writes the ID to the clipboard and reads «Скопійовано» with a check for 2 s. The announcement, profile.copiedStatus in a role=status line, is #192’s and not drawn. The ID is the one Sentry, Ghostfolio and Firefly III show.

T4 · Sign out everywhere

An outline «Вийти всюди…» opens the confirmation, whose filled «Продовжити» is the screen’s one CTA. Re-authentication follows it, the terminate half of ASVS 5.0 7.5.2; its view half is unmet, since Cognito lists no sessions. The re-authentication’s form is #494’s, shared with #317’s deletion, and not drawn here.

T5 · The ways in

The address is the link. It keeps its row’s geometry, 36 px and 11 px, and takes the pill’s states: hover sb-item-hover-bg, current sb-item-active-bg with the 2 px sb-indicator inset, bold and sb-icon-active. Its accessible name is «Профіль: » and the address, drawn as its aria-label, and its title keeps the whole address, as the row does today. On /profile Settings is not current. The rail gains a CircleUser RailItem after Settings, the address in its tooltip and in its name; its foot grows by one box, so Settings rises to 772 and «Вийти» stays at 856. Signed out, the slot holds as the glyph alone, no link and no hover, beside the panel’s status row; while the session is unknown it is held empty and inert, as the account rows are; so Settings stands at 772 signed in, signed out and before the session is known (T7). The drawer carries the panel’s link, its hit area 239,33 × 44 through TAP_44.

T6 · GET /me in flight, and failed

In flight past 300 ms, the page holds every box: the titles stand, each value is «—», the passkeys are three blocks, and «Копіювати», «До налаштувань» and «Вийти всюди…» are blocks of their own boxes: nothing in the held page can be reached. A failed read shows the failed screen (ScreenFailure) with the shell kept and the address current, as every screen’s first read does (Not found and failures). Signed out, /profile renders nothing in main and sends no GET /me, as a screen behind the view does; while the session is unknown it waits as the shell alone.

T7 · The geometry, measured on this sheet

T10 · Refused, and not drawn

  • A sign-in history. Cognito keeps one only under threat protection, «available in the Plus feature plan», and «You can’t use threat protection with federated sign-in».
  • A sessions list. No Cognito API lists a user’s sessions.
  • The current session’s method as a fact. The amr claim «is rolling out gradually»; the one place the page names the session’s method is the passkey card, because the relay cannot list passkeys for a Google sign-in (Auth model).
  • «Пошта підтверджена». OIDC makes email_verified optional, and the address is a label (Auth model).
  • An avatar or an account menu, rejected by name in Two shells, one breakpoint.
  • Creating, renaming or removing a passkey, deleting the account (#317), and the privacy notice at /apply (#495), which the page links once it exists, as the owner ruled.
  • The re-authentication step after «Продовжити» (#494).
