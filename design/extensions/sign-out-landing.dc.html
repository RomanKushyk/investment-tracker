The signed-out sentence — /sign-in says so, once, after a sign-out

Drawn from brief #279. A sign-out that finished lands on the address step with one line above the title — «Ви вийшли з системи.» — on that arrival and no other; submitting removes the words and holds their line. Ukrainian in every frame but one, as the app defaults.

T1 · The arrival

The address step as a sign-out leaves it — the shell’s «Вийти» or an answer’s «Вийти», both landing on /sign-in. One line opens the card, above the title: the circle-check at 16 in info, 8, then the sentence at 13 / 19,5 in ink, and 16 under the line to the title — the step T4 and T5 of auth-surface.dc.html leave under the glyph that opens an answer. The line is role="status"; nothing takes focus, and the address field takes it only after «Змінити», as today. The card’s top edge is where it always is: 64 below the bar at ≥ md, 16 at 360. The last frame is English.

T2 · Beside today

A visit that no sign-out caused — the URL typed, /apply’s way back, a reload, Back to the entry — draws today’s step, byte for byte: no line and nothing reserved for one. On a sign-out’s arrival everything under the line sits 35,5 lower (19,5 + 16), the card’s top edge unmoved. That offset is fixed from the first paint: the line’s box is laid out with the page and its words enter one frame later, inside it.

plain visit: no line, nothing reservedafter a sign-out: +35,5 under the line

T3 · After a submit — the line held

Submitting the address step removes the words; their line stays, empty, until the step changes, so «Продовжити» — pressed, checking — does not rise under the pointer. A refusal that keeps the step keeps the empty line with it; the refusal goes where it always goes, on the field’s reserved line, and neither moves the button. When the next step comes (the passkey or the password), or the address step again by «Змінити», there is no line.

T4 · Which arrivals say it

The fact rides the navigation as router state, and router state is history.state, which the browser hands back on Back/Forward and keeps across a reload — some browsers even across a restart (MDN, History: pushState()). Reading it once is not enough, so the page uses it up: the moment it arrives it replaces its own history entry with one that carries nothing, and keeps what it read for that visit. GOV.UK asks the same of its green banner: "Remove a green notification banner when the user moves to a new page."

T6 · Colour

T7 · Refused

  • A toast. web.dev asks for a page that shows the signed-out state; a toast leaves on a timer, and here a toast reports on the page the user stays on, as the failed sign-out does.
  • A tinted banner box. A radius and a height for one line, and info-tint a second informational surface for the same fact.
  • role="alert", or focus on the line. Nothing went wrong, and 4.1.3 asks for no change of focus. GOV.UK’s green banner takes both because it follows a submit on a full page load.
  • The slot under «Продовжити». Where a sentence about the step sits (auth-surface.dc.html T2); the owner ruled the line above the title.
  • Closing the line on submit. It would lift «Продовжити» under the pointer that pressed it.
  • The line on /apply after «Sign out and apply». That page is about applying; a confirmation there wants its own drawing.
