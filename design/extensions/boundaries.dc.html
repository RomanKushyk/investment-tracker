Not found and failures — the app’s own, in place of the router’s fallback

Drawn from brief #278. An unknown path shows «Сторінку не знайдено» inside the portfolio shell; a screen that throws shows «Не вдалося показати сторінку» in its place, the sidebar still working; and when the shell itself or the boot fails, the same words stand in the signed-out frame. T7 · Refused

  • The error’s message, a status code or a stack. OWASP’s Error Handling: “error details are logged … and not returned to the user”; the console already has them twice, from the router and from React.
  • “404”, “oops”, red. GOV.UK rules out “technical jargon like 404”, “informal or humorous words like oops” and “red text to warn people”.
  • A “Try again” that re-renders in place. With no loaders to revalidate, a location change is the only reset this app’s router has, a render that threw once usually throws again on the same data, and a reload also drops the in-memory caches a re-render keeps.
  • A “Back” button. No link in the app leads to a path that does not exist, so not found is reached by a typed, pasted or stale address, and the history behind it may be another site; GOV.UK’s pages have none.
  • Not found outside the shell. The sidebar is how a reader gets anywhere else, and the issue asks for it to keep working.
  • The screen’s own header for the in-shell states. The owner ruled the answer’s card, so the failed screen and the failed app are one block.
  • The card at the column’s top-left, where a screen’s own content starts. The owner ruled T3’s placement, so the three states sit alike in both frames.
  • The card centred down the column too. Drawn and set aside for T3’s placement, the owner’s final ruling; top-anchored, the card also keeps the shells’ rule that a centred card moves its button when its height changes (Two shells, one breakpoint).
