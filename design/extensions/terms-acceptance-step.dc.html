Quirenote · design extension · brief #554 · implementing #553

The terms acceptance step, and the terms rewritten for it

Brief: #554, as a comment on the issue. Implementing task: #553. Every frame is the running app, its answer screen redrawn into the step and its /terms and /privacy holding the rewritten sentences, serialized from computed styles, so a value here is a value the app already renders.

T1 · The step, at rest

The refused caller’s card, asking for one press. The step takes the place of the page asked for, in SignedOutShell’s bar and card, as the four answers do, so no portfolio and no app shell mount behind it. From the top: the muted 24 px glyph (lucide scroll-text), the title, the lead, «Ви увійшли як …»; then, 22 under that, the acceptance line, 12/18 muted, naming «Прийняти умови» and linking /terms alone in a new tab; 8 under it «Прийняти умови», filled; 10 under that the outline «Відхилити й вийти»; 12 under it the first layer, 12/18 muted: what is recorded and why, in the words of /privacy’s purpose (T4), linking /privacy in a new tab. Under the card, «Конфіденційність · Умови», which open a new tab here as under every answer. Accepting shows the page that was asked for, with no second sign-in. Declining signs out and lands on /sign-in with «Ви вийшли із застосунку.», as an answer’s «Вийти» does.

T2 · The step’s states

Nothing under the pointer moves. A failed write: the alert enters 12 under «Відхилити й вийти», 11/16,5 neg, role="alert", and the first layer moves down under it; both buttons stay where they were and take a press again. Its sentence names the refusal: another version, «Умови користування змінилися. Перезавантажте сторінку, щоб прочитати їх, і прийміть їх ще раз.», which a tab held open across a release meets; throttled, /apply’s «Зараз забагато запитів. Спробуйте ще раз за мить.»; anything else, «Не вдалося прийняти умови. Спробуйте ще раз.». «Прийняти умови» pressed: it reads «Приймаємо…» in the house busy tone until the write answers. «Відхилити й вийти» pressed: it holds its label in the busy tone while the sign-out runs, as an answer’s «Вийти» does. While one runs, the other keeps its look and a press on it does nothing until the first answers, as an answer’s two buttons do (Answer.tsx, the first line of leave).

T3 · /terms: the intro, §1 and §5 rewritten

Three sentences, the rest as shipped. The intro names both buttons and when the terms start to apply on each route. §1 keeps the operator’s status and gives the right to refuse an account as the reason the terms are no public offer. §5’s first bullet keeps only its second sentence, the declined application’s rule, word for word. Each frame stands at the section, 16 under the viewport’s top, as a contents link places it.

T4 · /privacy: the contract purpose names the record

One bullet of «Навіщо й на якій підставі». Its contract purpose also names keeping the record of the agreement, which version of the terms was accepted and when, under the basis it already cites (Law 2297-VI Art. 11 ч. 1 п. 3; GDPR Art. 6(1)(b)). The data section already lists that record for the account (src/i18n/messages.ts:2635). The step’s first layer states the same purpose. Every other sentence is as shipped, and so is «Останнє оновлення»: neither /privacy nor /terms has reached production, and the release that ships them sets both dates. Each frame stands at the section, as a contents link places it.
