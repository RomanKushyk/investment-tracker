Quirenote · design extension · brief #497 · implementing #495

The privacy notice: /privacy, /apply’s first layer, and the link on every page

Brief: #497, as a comment on the issue. Implementing task: #495; the retention the notice states is enforced first by #525. Every frame is the running app with the new surfaces built from its own class recipes and serialized from computed styles, so a value here is a value the app already renders.

T1 · /privacy, for every reader

The signed-out shell, its card at the document’s measure, whoever reads it. A page with no portfolio behind it mounts neither app shell (Two shells, one breakpoint), so a signed-in reader sees the same page and returns with Back; nothing waits on the session and no shell swaps. The bar and the language control are the shell’s; the card keeps its radius 24 and padding 28 (22 below md) and takes max-width: 680px, so a line of the 13 px body holds about 80 characters (WCAG 1.4.8). «До Quirenote» first, an explicit way back to / above the title, since a reader who came from a bookmark or an e-mail has no history to go Back through and the bar’s mark is no link (NN/g, Homepage links). Then the title, «Останнє оновлення», the two intro paragraphs, «Зміст» with an anchor per section, then nine numbered sections; a section lands 16 below the viewport’s top. The cookie list is one panel of rows, stacked at every width, so nothing scrolls sideways at 360. Under the card, centred, the link every signed-out page carries (S3), naming the page here.

The full-length frames are the whole notice; the English one says no more than the Ukrainian (Law 2704-VIII Art. 27(6)).

T2 · /apply’s first layer

Under the button, 12 px from it: two sentences and the link, always visible. Who uses the address and why, the check that could surprise (Cloudflare’s), and where the rest is: «Політиці конфіденційності» → /privacy (WP260 ¶36; the CNIL’s Niveau 1), opening in a new tab as the widget’s own links on this form do, so the typed address and the passed check stay; its name says so to assistive technology («відкривається в новій вкладці»). It names Quirenote, not the owner, by the owner’s ruling; the controller’s name is one link away. Muted, 12/18, never a checkbox and never “you agree” (EDPB 2/2019 ¶20). A step alert enters between the button and the layer: the button never moves, the layer moves down under it. «Вже маєте доступ? Увійти» follows, as today.

On the version’s line: «Конфіденційність» · the version. 11 px in sb-item, underlined, sb-item-hover under the pointer; the dot and the version keep sb-label.
