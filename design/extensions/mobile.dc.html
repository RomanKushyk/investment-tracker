Phase 6 acceptance — what this file delivers
  • S1–S6 drawn in the master reference's idiom: <x-dc>, all styles inline, every colour and size literal, no runtime script.
  • Every surface at 360 and at 768; the drawer open and closed; the keyboard-open state of / drawn.
  • --color-scrim minted with both values and its separation re-derived — 5.23 : 1 in light, and the finding that dark needs an edge instead (1.02 : 1 without one).
  • No radius contradicts D56. The two recomputed values are shown with their arithmetic; two full-bleed bars take square corners with the reason stated; the ScrollArea thumb is recomputed from shadcn's 8 px rather than inheriting its capsule.
  • Every width claim checked against Ukrainian: 153.9 px in a 220 px drawer box, 44.6 px day cells at 312 px.
  • No D5-pinned figure moves — every number was read from production on 2026-08-14.
