Quirenote · design extension · brief #297 · implementing #298 and #299

A bot check above the button, on the three forms that open a door

Drawn from brief #297. Cloudflare Turnstile in Managed mode sits between the field and the submit button on /apply, the sign-in address step and the password step. It takes the field's width at 1280 and its 300 minimum at 360. Its slot is reserved, so the button stands at one place whatever the widget shows, and a sentence the check gives sits under the button.

T1 · /apply

The application form: one field, the widget, «Подати заявку». #298 verifies the token before anything else, so a refusal answers before the database is asked. A refused token reads «Перевірку безпеки не завершено. Спробуйте ще раз.» under the button, as «Зараз забагато запитів. Спробуйте ще раз за мить.» does today; the field keeps its own line for the address.

Five states, the button at one place in all of them (T7): verifying, «Перевірка…», what every visit sees first, the button live; passed, «Успіх!», what most visits see; the checkbox, «Підтвердьте, що ви людина», when Managed judges the visit risky; failed, the widget's own «Помилка перевірки» or a token the server refused, with «Перевірку безпеки не завершено. Спробуйте ще раз.»; could not load, the slot empty and «Не вдалося завантажити перевірку безпеки. Перевірте мережу чи блокувальник реклами й оновіть сторінку.». The address is drawn filled wherever a press came first. Each at 1280 and 360, light and dark, Ukrainian first and then English.

T4 · Pressed before the token

The button is never gated. The field is checked first: an empty or malformed address, or an empty password, is refused on its own line, as today, and nothing waits for a token. On /apply and the password step a valid press while the widget is still verifying shows the step's busy word, «Надсилаємо…» or «Входимо…», disabled at 70 % as every busy button is, and the request goes when the token comes. On the address step the same press is refused with «Перевірка безпеки ще триває. Спробуйте ще раз за мить.» under the button, the button stays live and nothing is sent; the next press after the token starts sign-in. Its /auth/start opens the passkey sheet, which Safari before 17.4 opens only after one fetch and no other wait (Auth model), so nothing may wait before that fetch. Wikipedia's hCaptcha answers an early press the same way: "We're not quite finished loading the form. Please wait a few seconds, then try again." The wait ends with the widget's own answer and with no clock of the app's: the token sends the request; a failure, or the widget's own timeout, returns the step live with «Перевірку безпеки не завершено. Спробуйте ще раз.» under the button; the checkbox returns it live, so the press asked for is the checkbox's. A script that neither loads nor fails gives no answer to wait for; #298 detects it and shows the widget as could not load.

When a sentence appears. The widget's own failure puts «Перевірку безпеки не завершено. Спробуйте ще раз.» up when it happens, press or not, since the button cannot succeed until the check does. The widget's next «Успіх!» clears it. A token the server refused puts it up after the press. With the widget never loaded, a press shows «Не вдалося завантажити перевірку безпеки. Перевірте мережу чи блокувальник реклами й оновіть сторінку.», again on every press, and sends nothing. Every answer spends the token, and the widget is reset for the next press: a second press after «Неправильна адреса або пароль.», «Забагато спроб. Спробуйте пізніше.» or any refusal carries a fresh token, so a spent token never reads as «Перевірку безпеки не завершено. Спробуйте ще раз.». Drawn in light, Ukrainian, at 1280 and 360.

T9 · Refused, and not drawn

  • Invisible mode: Cloudflare makes referencing its Turnstile Privacy Addendum a condition, and Managed gives the same protection with the widget in view.
  • Interaction-only, shown when needed: under the button it sits below the action it gates; above it, the button drops 65 under a resting pointer (Interaction rules).
  • A gated button, Cloudflare's tutorial's: GOV.UK, "Disabled buttons have poor contrast and can confuse some users, so avoid them if possible".
  • The compact widget at 360: 150 × 140, half the field's width and more than twice the 65 the other sizes take.
  • A narrower card padding at 360: it would move every auth page to fit one control.
  • A privacy line under the widget: Managed mode requires none, and the widget carries its own «Конфіденційність» link. Lichess and MailerLite, both on Turnstile in the EU, show none.
  • A sentence inside the widget's slot: every step-level sentence sits under its button (auth-surface.dc.html).
  • A widget on Google's way in: /auth/google/begin is out of #299's scope.
  • The passkey step's «Спробувати ще раз», NOT DRAWN rather than refused: it starts sign-in again with a fresh /auth/start (passkeyFirst), from a step that holds no widget, so under #299 it needs a token this drawing does not place. #299 settles it, through a brief of its own if it needs anything on the card.
  • The first sign-in's «Встановіть пароль», NOT DRAWN rather than refused: its setNewPassword proves the temporary password with a fresh /auth/start (src/auth/sign-in.ts), from a step this drawing gives no widget. #299 settles it, through a brief of its own if it needs anything on the card.
  • The test sitekeys' red "for testing only" strip: it appears only with Cloudflare's dummy keys.
