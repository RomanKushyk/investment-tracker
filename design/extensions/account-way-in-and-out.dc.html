A sign-out that leaves, and a sign-in that arrives

Drawn from brief #445. A sign-out sets out to leave its page with «Вийти» busy, and the slot answers signed out only when the page is still here after a later act of the user's. Back from Google, the card says «Входимо…» instead of showing the empty form again. Ukrainian in every frame but three, as the app defaults.

T1 · The way out, in the panel

(a) Pressed. «Вийти» goes busy at once: aria-disabled at 70 %, never disabled, so focus stays on it for a retry (sign-out.dc.html T1). The route's screen still stands. (b) Leaving. The relay has revoked the session, so the screens have dropped the account's figures, the capital reads «—», and the address row reads «Ви увійшли» because the session no longer has an address. «Вийти» stays busy while the page is on its way out, to /sign-in or through Cognito's logout. (c) Still here. When the page is still here after a later act of the user's, Back to it, a Stop or a move to another route, and by the next route change at the latest: «Ви не ввійшли» and «Увійти», as sign-in-entry.dc.html draws them. Where the redirect still takes the page after that act, (c) stands until it goes. The three share one slot, so Settings stands still across them (T7). Every account control in the shell shows the same state: one mounted while the page leaves, after a collapse to the rail or across 768, shows (b) too (#446).

(a) is drawn in light, lifted from sign-in-entry.dc.html's signed-in frame with the portfolio still loaded; its dark rows are (b)'s with the address in place of «Ви увійшли».

T2 · The rail

The rail holds no address, so (a) and (b) differ only behind the item: the busy RailBox, aria-disabled at 70 %, named «Вийти» by its aria-label. In (c) it is «Увійти», a link, as sign-in-entry.dc.html T2 draws it.

T3 · The drawer

The panel's rows at the drawer's step, 8, at a true 360. The busy «Вийти» keeps its 44-tall TAP_44 overlay. Nothing presses through it while it is busy, and in (c) the overlay is «Увійти»'s, as today.

Unsaved work does not hold a sign-out, the owner's ruling. By the time any guard could ask, the relay has revoked the session, and the work goes with the account's figures, so «Продовжити редагування» could keep nothing. A guard that asked before the revoke was weighed and declined.

The mechanism is #449's, the owner's ruling after three review rounds. Which browser signals tell that the page is still here, what the tab must remember for the logout's landing and what the shell may forget, and how a control mounted while the page leaves learns it (#446) are derived and tested in code, against the facts the rounds measured, which #449 carries. A drawing can only reason about those paths; the code can run them (Design pipeline: "The drawing owns the RESULT and the code owns the mechanism").

A logout halted before Cognito answers leaves Cognito's own session. The relay revoked the app's session before the redirect began, so the app is signed out and (c) is true of it. Cognito's session cookie, which only its logout clears (leaveThroughLogout, src/auth/app.ts), stays where the halt left it, as it does today. A halt on the way back to /sign-in comes too late to keep it: Cognito's logout endpoint "signs out the user and redirects". This drawing changes what the slot says, not what the halt leaves.

Back shows nothing private. web.dev's Back/forward cache asks that user state be "updated or cleared after a page is restored from bfcache". Here the figures were dropped before the page left, as (b) draws, so the slot is all that is updated.

A second press. On (a) and (b), «Вийти» does nothing: it is busy, and every press joins the one sign-out. On (c), «Увійти» is a plain visit to /sign-in, without «Ви вийшли з системи.», which only a sign-out's own arrival says (sign-out-landing.dc.html). A logout that lands on /sign-in after the user moved to another route is such an arrival, and still says it.

T9 · Refused, and not drawn

  • «Увійти» in answer to the press on «Вийти»: the press asked to leave, and it never brings «Увійти» (sign-in-entry.dc.html T4, Interaction rules). Only a later act of the user's does, Back, a Stop or a route change, and a Stop by keyboard can come while the pointer still rests on the pill.
  • A busy «Вийти» on a page still here after the user's later act: the slot would state a session that has ended (NN/g's visibility of system status). (b) states it only while the page is on its way out, by the next route change at the latest (#447); changing it sooner would put «Увійти» under the pointer that pressed «Вийти».
  • Sending the logout again after a Stop: the halt is the user's own act, and the page would leave against it (T4).
  • Unsaved work holding a sign-out, and a dialog before it: the owner's ruling (T4).
  • A timer that ends the busy state: no clock knows whether a redirect is still loading.
  • A reload on Back: the page restored holds no figures to clear (T4).
  • The address step on the callback, today's: an empty form after the user chose Google, its «Продовжити» pressable while the relay is still completing the Google sign-in.
  • The bar alone, no card, declined by the owner: a cold relay leaves the page blank for seconds, with nothing saying the system is working.
  • The portfolio's placeholder on the callback, declined by the owner: a refused return would show the shell to a caller who is not signed in, then drop to /sign-in.
  • A spinner: the app's busy state is a word with an ellipsis — «Входимо…», «Зберігаємо…», «Надсилаємо…» — and its one spinning glyph is the fetch button's own.
  • The status card in place of the passkey step: a step swapped as the sheet closes replaces what lies under the pointer that closed it.
