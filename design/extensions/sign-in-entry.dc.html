«Увійти» in the account slot — the panel, the rail and the drawer

Drawn from brief #440. Signed out, the account slot keeps the shape it has signed in: «Ви не ввійшли» where the address stands, then «Увійти», a link to /sign-in, where «Вийти» stands. The rail shows «Увійти» alone. Ukrainian in every frame but one, as the app defaults.

T1 · The panel

Answered signed out, the slot holds two rows at the geometry the signed-in rows have. First the status, «Ви не ввійшли», where the address stands: 36 tall, the CircleUser glyph at 16 in sb-icon, the words at 11 px in sb-item, not pressable. Then «Увійти», where «Вийти» stands: a pill in Settings' recipe, the LogIn glyph at 16 in sb-icon, the label at 13,5 in sb-item, r9, no fill at rest, 3 apart. It is a link to /sign-in, because it goes to a route; «Вийти» stays a button. It is a plain Link and never a NavLink, unlike Settings: /sign-in mounts no shell, but /sign-in/x falls to the shell's * route, where a NavLink would mark it current — so it takes no tint, no indicator and no aria-current. Hover takes the nav's idle-hover pair, sb-item-hover-bg and sb-item-hover on the label, the glyph keeping sb-icon. Focus is the app's one base ring, 2 px focus at offset 2, following the link's r9.

The held frame, the signed-in frame and the signed-out frame stand side by side so Settings can be read across them: it does not move (T4).

T2 · The rail

The rail holds no address, so it has no status row: its slot is one item in every state. Signed out that item is «Увійти»: RailBox, the LogIn glyph at 18 in a 40 × 36 box at r9, 6 under Settings, hover only and no press scale, as its neighbours. It is a plain Link, not RailItem's NavLink: /sign-in mounts no shell, but /sign-in/x falls to the shell's * route, where a NavLink would mark it current — so it takes no tint, no indicator and no aria-current. Its name is its aria-label, «Увійти»; the tooltip shows the same words 16 to the right, and Tooltip hides its own text from the description, so the name is read once. The tooltip's words are drawn at 12 / 18: text-[12px] sets no line height, so they inherit 1.5 (sign-out.dc.html drew 16).

T3 · The drawer

The panel's two rows in the drawer's band, at the step the band takes below 768, 8. «Увійти» draws 239 × 36,25 (239,33 as read here, T5) and its TAP_44 overlay is 44 tall, 3,875 past each edge. Settings carries the same overlay, and between the two lie two steps of 8 and the status row, which presses nothing, so the overlays stay 44,25 apart and no tap lands on both — the arithmetic «Вийти» has today. Hover does not exist under touch, so the drawer draws focus and not hover. The English frame closes this section.

T4 · The held slot and the three answers

The relay is asked once on every portfolio load. Until it answers, the slot is held, empty and inert, at the signed-in rows' height. Answered signed in, the address and «Вийти» fade into it, as today. Answered signed out, the status and «Увійти» fade into it, where today the slot folds shut. Left unanswered (offline), it stays held until the next ask is answered. On a host with no auth environment there is nothing to ask and nothing to sign in to, so the slot is closed from the first render and never held — nothing moves there either. A sign-out that succeeded keeps the address and «Вийти» until the route changes, as sign-out.dc.html rules, although the session is already signed out: the status and «Увійти» appear only when a load is answered signed out, never under the pointer that pressed «Вийти».

Held, main is first-paint.dc.html's placeholder screen, which this sheet does not redraw, so the held frames leave it blank; signed out it is empty, as the app draws it today.

The cost, stated: signed out the band is now the signed-in band, so the nav above it starts to scroll at the taller viewport the signed-in shell already has — below 793,75 in the panel and 626 in the rail, where a signed-out shell scrolls below 715,5 and 584 today (sign-out.dc.html T5).

T7 · Refused, and not drawn

  • The slot folding shut on a signed-out answer, today's behaviour — it moves Settings by 78,25, 42 and 88,25 under a resting pointer (Interaction rules).
  • A blank row above «Увійти» — none of fourteen surveyed products ships one; the owner ruled the status row.
  • «Гість» — no surveyed product uses it, and the app has no guest mode; and any wording with «систем», which #318 rules out of the Ukrainian auth copy.
  • An Apply control in the shell — /sign-in's card is one link from «Подати заявку» (#285).
  • A button for a route — the entry is a link (APG; Roselli).
  • A person glyph on the entry — it would read as the profile the status row stands for; the arrow mirrors the sign-out's.
  • «Вхід» — a noun, where the control it replaces in the slot, «Вийти», is a verb.
  • What main shows signed out — not drawn here: #194's landing and #137's demo. It stays empty.
