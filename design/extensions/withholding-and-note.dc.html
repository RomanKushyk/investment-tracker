The withholding and the note on the transaction row

Eight frames, drawn at 1280 and at a true 360 in both themes · mints nothing · supersedes geometry in where-things-live.dc.html S4 and the type list in metrics-exposure.dc.html S10, and edits neither.

T1 · What the row is at 360, and the finding that decides the shape

Read on the running app. The row is a single flex line with five children and it is exactly full: 280 − 194 of content − 32 of gaps leaves the label 54, and «Реінвестиція · REIT» is nineteen characters shown in about seven. The label is already truncated before anything is added. That is the whole argument for a second line — there is no budget on the first one, and taking any would truncate a label that is already truncating.

The row is 60 px tall at 360 and 36 at 1280, and the difference is not type: the text is 18,75 in both, and what grows is the ✕, which takes its 44 × 44 hit area below the breakpoint. 44 is hit area, never geometry — so the note's line is measured against the text, not against the control that happens to be tallest.

Three more boxes are measured rather than assumed, because a sheet that says “measured” owes every figure in it. Both cards carry radius 24, which is Card's own and neither branch of the shape rule — it is a surface, and surfaces keep the reference's 16 / 20 / 24. The form card's side padding is 23, derived as (card − content) / 2 rather than typed, because typing both is how a drawing ends up two pixels wider than the card it claims to be. And the two cards at 360 are the same 336 wide with different inner rhythms — 23 against 28: the ledger's is not padding at all but the Scroller's rail gutter, which is why it is wider and why it is not a number this sheet is free to pick.

The select says «Виплата відсотків» and the row says «Купон» for the same type, and that is shipped rather than a slip — the dictionary carries recentCoupon beside types.interest_payout deliberately. Both are drawn below so the next reader does not file it. The refusals take ONE noun phrase for one field: the label reads «Утримано податку», so the sentences read «Утриманий податок…» rather than inventing a third word for the same sum.

T2 · Where the withholding goes — one rule, a second occupant

The panel already has a rule for its two-column row, and this file does not write a new one. Today: with units, [Units][Amount] and «Джерело коштів» full width beneath; without them, [Amount][Source] on one line. On a payout the withholding takes that second column — [Amount][Withheld] — and the source drops to its own row exactly as units already make it drop. Amount first, then what was taken out of it, because that is the sentence the row records and the order the person reads it in.

The alternative is drawn nowhere because it fails on the rule rather than on taste. Putting the withholding on the LEFT, in the units slot, makes the two-column row's first column mean “units” on four types and “a deduction” on two — one column with two meanings, told apart only by which type is selected.

WHAT IT DOES NOT BUY IS A STILL AMOUNT, and an earlier draft of this file claimed it did. Measured on the running app: the amount sits in column TWO on a buy and column ONE on a payout, which is the behaviour it already has between a buy and a deposit — the cells are placed in document order and the row's first occupant decides. So the amount changes column with the type here as it always has; what the placement settles is which FIELD it is paired with, and that a deduction never occupies the column units occupy.

T3 · The form, four frames

Card 365.39 wide at 1280 and 336 at 360, content 319.39 and 290, padding 20 / 22, radius 24. Fields are 36 rendered at radius 9 on field-border, label 11 px on muted, the submit 36 at radius 9 above the breakpoint and 44 at 11 below it — the proportional rule, round(min(w,h) × 0.26), not a capsule anywhere. Field type is 13 px at 1280 and 16 below md, which is the app's own rule and not this sheet's.

T5 · Why the cap is 100, and why it is counted in characters

Measured: 100 characters is three lines and 48,00 px at 360, taking a record from 61 to 111,00 — near double. The line is drawn at 11 / 16, not at the row's own 12,5 / 18,75, and an earlier draft of this paragraph printed 56,25 by using the row's figure for the note's own line.

The face is monospace, so for a given SAMPLE the count decides the wrap and the language does not — the same hundred characters measure the same three lines in Ukrainian and in English, which is a property this app has and most do not. It is not a general law and this file does not claim one: wrapping is still word-based, so a hundred characters of four twenty-two character words is four lines at 280. What the count fixes is the WIDTH a line can hold; where the breaks fall is the sample's.

EVERY BASE ABOVE IS A BORDERED RECORD, and saying so is not pedantry: the FIRST row takes first:border-t-0 and is one pixel shorter, so 60 → 110 there. A draft of this table paired a bordered bare row against a border-less noted one and printed 61 → 110, which is neither. The note costs the height of its own line plus the 2 px above it, which is +50 at 360 and +34 at 1280 — a draft printed the 360 figure as though it held everywhere, on the same page as the 1280 row that disproves it.

Three lines is a note; a fourth is a paragraph hanging under a 60 px row. That is the whole of it, and it is drawn above rather than argued. The cap counts CHARACTERS and not lines for a reason outside this sheet: transaction_note_ck has to enforce the same bound, and SQL cannot check a line count. So the drawing picks the line count it wants and the store gets the number that produces it at the NARROW shell, which is the binding one — a cap chosen at 1280 would be two lines there and four or five at 360.

THE SECOND LINE DOES NOT RESERVE THE ✕ COLUMN, and this file's first draft did. Measured, that reservation cost a fourth line at 360 — 228 usable instead of 280, 64 px instead of 48, the row at 126 rather than 110 — and it bought nothing, because the ✕ is a child of the FIRST line and is centred on it. A second line running the row's full width passes under no control at all. The figure is kept because it is the one that would have moved the cap: reserve that column and 100 characters stops being three lines, so the number and the layout are not independent choices.

T7 · Motion, and what this sheet refuses

No new motion. The withholding's group reveals and exits through the app's one Reveal at 300 ms on the soft curve, the refusal fades in at 200, and both inherit the global prefers-reduced-motion kill-switch. The specification is in this file's comment, where motion specifications live.

Three things are refused by name. A <textarea> for the note: a hundred characters is a line, not a paragraph, and a control at any height but 36 is invisible to the structural walk that holds every field edge in this app — the guard would silently stop covering the newest field on the screen. A character counter beside the note: it is furniture for a bound the field can simply refuse at, and it would be the only counter in the app. And a breakdown of the withholding into its two Ukrainian components: the provider reports one figure, so two fields would be derived from rates, and a computed figure eventually lies where a recorded one cannot.
