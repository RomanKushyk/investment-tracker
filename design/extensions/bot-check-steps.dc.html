Quirenote · design extension · brief #465 · implementing #299

A widget for the passkey retry, and none for the first password

Drawn from brief #465. The passkey step's «Спробувати ще раз» gets its token from a Turnstile widget that enters with the retry, between «Вхід ключем доступу не завершено…» and the link. «Встановіть пароль» answers the challenge the password step already holds, so it starts nothing and takes no widget; when that challenge has expired, the invitee is back on the password step, with the address kept and one new sentence.

T1 · The passkey step, the retry offered

After the OS sheet is cancelled or refused, the step says «Вхід ключем доступу не завершено: його скасовано, або на цьому пристрої немає ключа для цієї адреси.» under «Увійти з паролем», as auth-surface.dc.html T2 draws it. The widget enters with that sentence, 12 below it, and «Спробувати ще раз» stands 16 below the widget, the gap its 44 × 44 hit area below md is owed, (44 − 19,5) / 2 = 12,25 past the link's 19,5 box (tap-target.ts): the widget sits above the action it gates, as bot-check.dc.html places it above every button it gates. Everything enters below both buttons, so nothing the pointer rests on moves when the OS sheet closes. A passkey sign-in that succeeds never renders a widget on this step. A sentence the check gives sits 12 under the link, the action it gates; «Вхід ключем доступу не завершено…» keeps its place under «Увійти з паролем».

Five states, everything above the check's own sentence at one place in all of them (T6): verifying, «Перевірка…», the link live; passed, «Успіх!»; the checkbox, «Підтвердьте, що ви людина»; failed, the widget's own «Помилка перевірки» or a token the server refused, with «Перевірку безпеки не завершено. Спробуйте ще раз.» under the link; could not load, the slot empty at 65 and «Не вдалося завантажити перевірку безпеки. Перевірте мережу чи блокувальник реклами й оновіть сторінку.» under the link. The widget is reset after every answer, so a sheet refused again offers the retry with a fresh widget, verifying. Tab order: «Увійти з паролем», the widget, the link. Each at 1280 and 360, light and dark, Ukrainian first and then English.

T2 · The retry pressed, before the token and with it

Before the token, the press sends nothing and «Перевірка безпеки ще триває. Спробуйте ще раз за мить.» appears under the link, as on the address step (bot-check.dc.html T4); the link stays live, and the next press after the token starts the retry. With the token, the press sends /auth/start at once, with nothing awaited before the fetch: in Safari before 17.4 WebKit carries a click's gesture only through a fetch or XHR, for at most 10 s, and the OS sheet opens only inside that gesture. The sentence, the slot and the link leave together, and the step stands as at its arrival while the sheet opens (auth-surface.dc.html T2, t2-passkey-*). Drawn in light, Ukrainian, at 1280 and 360.

T3 · «Встановіть пароль» on the held challenge

The step answers the NEW_PASSWORD_REQUIRED challenge the password step's answer carried, which that step won under its own token. It sends no /auth/start, so it takes no widget and stands exactly as auth-surface.dc.html T5 draws it, «Зберегти й продовжити» at T5's place. A refused password, «Щонайменше 8 символів: велика й мала літери, цифра та символ.» or «Це тимчасовий пароль. Придумайте власний.», is answered as T5 and password-reuse.dc.html draw it, on the same challenge: measured on the dev pool, the challenge answered a valid password after both refusals. Pressed, the button reads «Зберігаємо…», disabled at 70 %, as today. Back on this step after the expired session's return (T4), the new password the invitee had typed is filled in, so after the temporary password is proven again they press once more (WCAG 2.2.5, "the user can continue the activity without loss of data after re-authenticating"). It is the same frame as the step's own, so it is drawn at 1280 and 360, in light, Ukrainian.

T4 · The session expired: back on the password step

The challenge lives three minutes, Cognito's default, which infra/ does not change; measured on the dev pool, an answer 200 s after the start was refused, "Invalid session for the user, session is expired.". The time limit is a security control of Cognito's, the kind WCAG 2.2.1's Understanding calls essential. A press after it has run out returns to the password step, as bot-check.dc.html draws that step, its widget verifying: the address kept, the password field empty and focused, wearing the app's :focus-visible ring, and «Час на встановлення пароля минув. Введіть тимчасовий пароль ще раз.» 12 under «Увійти». The temporary password is asked again, under 3.3.7's security exception; the widget gives the fresh start its token. Each at 1280 and 360, light and dark, Ukrainian and English.

T8 · Refused

  • A widget on the passkey step from its arrival: it would sit above «Увійти з паролем», which it does not gate; every passkey sign-in, the successful ones included, would show a third-party frame under the OS sheet; its token could expire while the sheet stays open.
  • A retry that returns to the address step: FIDO's messaging guidelines, GitHub's "Retry passkey" and Keycloak's "Try again" beside "Try Another Way" all retry in place, beside the fallback.
  • A retry that skips the check: Clerk, Turnkey, authentik and Logto skip a passkey sign-in, because the assertion cannot be guessed; here the start is what a flood costs, and OWASP and Supabase gate the start.
  • Deferring the challenge to the press, Turnstile's execution: execute: it puts a wait between the press and the fetch.
  • A widget on «Встановіть пароль»: an invitee would clear two checks in a row, and none of the products the brief checked gates the step after a successful password check (Keycloak, Auth0, Supabase; OWASP's change-password list).
  • Raising AuthSessionValidity: 15 minutes is its most and still a limit; it would lengthen every challenge the pool issues.
  • Clearing the typed new password on the return: WCAG 2.2.5.
